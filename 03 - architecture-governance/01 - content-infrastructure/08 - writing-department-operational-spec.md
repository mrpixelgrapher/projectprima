# 08 — Writing Department Operational Spec

Type: department spec

## Status

IN PROGRESS — established after Media Department completion, per sequential deployment model (Spec 07).

## Core Operating Philosophy

Knowledge Sufficiency: all writing originates from rigorous bottom-up research. Drafting is the final synthesis step, never the discovery mechanism. Research is the primary value-generating activity; AI is the amplification mechanism.

## Two-Stage Workflow

### Stage 1 — Research (Expansion)

| Activity | Output |
|---|---|
| Topic boundary mapping | List of all relevant sources, perspectives, frameworks |
| Source acquisition | Collected primary and secondary sources |
| Observation extraction | Recurring themes, contradictions, notable insights |
| Framework identification | Reusable mental models from accumulated research |
| Contextual density check | Gate: sufficient depth for original synthesis? |

**Exit gate:** Can the full boundary of the topic be named? Are there 3+ independent source categories? Does accumulated material support original synthesis (not just restatement)?

### Stage 2 — Drafting (Compression)

| Activity | Output |
|---|---|
| Core narrative identification | The single argument or insight this piece carries |
| Research synthesis | Draft emerges from accumulated material |
| Structure and flow | Subheadings, transitions, supporting evidence placement |
| Original insight formation | Claims not present in any single source |
| Quality review | Traceability check, terminology alignment, collapse rules |

## Quality Standards

- Every claim traceable to research artifacts
- Original insights distinguishable from source material
- Platform-appropriate register (Substack long-form vs. LinkedIn professional vs. Twitter concise)
- SEO-optimized (primary keyword + secondary keywords, internal linking)
- Visual assets integrated (custom graphics, framework diagrams)

## AI Integration

AI serves as amplification, not replacement:
- **Research phase:** AI assists source discovery, pattern extraction, framework identification
- **Drafting phase:** AI assists structure, synthesis, and formatting — but original insight comes from accumulated human + AI research
- **Quality phase:** AI assists traceability checking, terminology alignment, collapse rule enforcement
