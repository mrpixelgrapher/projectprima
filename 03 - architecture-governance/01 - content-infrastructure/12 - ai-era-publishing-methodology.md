# 12 — AI-Era Publishing Methodology

Type: methodology document (Gap 02)

## The Gap

Current publishing treats AI as the content engine. The correct model: AI amplifies research quality, not replaces it. The strategic window for AI-assisted publishing is finite (5-6 years before detection catches up).

## Core Principles

### 1. Finite Strategic Window

AI detection will improve. Techniques that produce low-detectability content today may fail within 5-6 years. The response: build the archive NOW while the leverage asymmetry is favorable.

**Action items:**
- Publish at least 5 well-researched pieces before the window shifts
- Each piece must be durable (evergreen, referenceable, SEO-stable)
- The archive compounds: each piece strengthens previous work's discoverability

### 2. Depth Over Volume

Differentiation comes from research depth, not generation speed. AI-assisted creators who publish fast but shallow will be caught by detection. Those who publish deep and research-backed will survive regardless of detection advances.

**Quality threshold:** Every published piece must contain at least one original insight not present in any single source. If all claims can be traced to a single article or web search, the piece is too shallow.

### 3. Research Artifacts as Permanent Assets

Every research effort produces artifacts that survive beyond the article:
- Source collections (reusable for future pieces on the same topic)
- Extracted frameworks (reusable across topics in the same domain)
- Synthesis notes (reasoning pathways for future reference)

These artifacts make the NEXT article on the same topic faster and deeper — longitudinal knowledge accumulation.

### 4. AI as Force Multiplier

AI should be positioned as:
- **Research amplifier:** Finding more sources, extracting patterns, identifying contradictions
- **Drafting accelerator:** Structuring and formatting accumulated research
- **Quality checker:** Traceability verification, terminology alignment, collapse rules

AI should NOT be positioned as:
- **Research replacement:** "Search the web and write about it" (this is the anti-pattern from Spec 01)
- **Content generator:** Producing text without accumulated research
- **Shortcut:** Reducing the research phase to speed up publication

## CE Integration

This methodology is enforced by:
- Gate 8 (KSE): Blocks execution when knowledge is insufficient
- Governance Rule 01: Research-first content production
- Cognitive cognitive sequence: Verifies research phase completion before drafting instructions
- Arena research: Serves as the expansion phase when KSE scores LOW/CRITICAL
