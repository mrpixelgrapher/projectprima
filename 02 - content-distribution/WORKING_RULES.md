# Working Rules — Content Distribution

## Rule 1: Translation, Not Syndication (mandatory)

Every platform derivative must be a NEW piece of content written in that platform's native register. Copy-pasting from Substack or cross-posting identical content is a governance violation.

**Test:** Would a native user of this platform find this content natural, or does it read like a repost? If repost → rewrite.

## Rule 2: Substack Is The Anchor (mandatory)

All distribution flows FROM Substack TO platforms, never the reverse. The Substack article is the canonical source. Platform derivatives are discoveries that funnel audience BACK to Substack.

- LinkedIn: link in first comment (algorithm deprioritizes external links in body)
- Twitter/X: thread ends with newsletter link
- Threads: direct newsletter link in post
- Medium: canonical link pointing to Substack
- Instagram: bio link to newsletter
- Facebook: direct link in post body

## Rule 3: Seed-Based Decomposition (mandatory)

Every derivative traces to a specific decomposition seed from the Writing Department output contract. No derivative is created without identifying which seed it derives from.

## Rule 4: 30-Day Deployment (mandatory)

Each article generates content across 4 weeks, not a single publication spike:

| Week | Focus | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Core insight exposure | Main derivatives on all platforms |
| 2 | Process deep-dive | Twitter thread, LinkedIn case study, Instagram carousel |
| 3 | Implications & discussion | Threads follow-up, LinkedIn poll, Facebook discussion |
| 4 | Framework application | Instagram visual, Twitter mini-thread, LinkedIn framework post |

## Rule 5: Platform-Native Quality (mandatory)

Each platform has specific formatting rules. Violations produce low engagement and audience distrust.

**LinkedIn:** First 3 lines are the hook (appear before "See more"). Line breaks every 2-3 sentences. Max 3-5 hashtags.

**Twitter/X:** First tweet is the hook (under 280 chars). Thread has clear progression. Final tweet links to full article.

**Threads:** Opens with the implication, not the process. Invites response. 1 strong graphic.

**Instagram:** Visual-first. Each carousel slide = one seed. Caption expands the visual.

**Medium:** Different title from Substack. Executive summary angle. 800-1500 words.

**Facebook:** Accessible language. Educational framing. Strong opening image. 300-600 words.

## Rule 6: Custom Graphics Required (mandatory)

Every derivative includes at least 1 custom graphic. Stock photos are prohibited. Graphics serve as intellectual compression mechanisms — complex concepts understood rapidly.
