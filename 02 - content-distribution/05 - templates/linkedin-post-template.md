# LinkedIn Post Template

## Post Type: [Thought Leadership / Case Study / Framework / Industry Commentary / Contrarian]

### Hook (first 3 lines — visible before "See more")

[Line 1: Bold claim or surprising result. Under 15 words.]

[Line 2: Context that makes the claim matter.]

[Line 3: Transition that makes the reader want to click "See more."]

### Body

[Evidence section — specific examples, data, or case references that support the hook.]

[Framework section — named model or mental model that explains the mechanism.]

[Application section — how the reader can apply this insight to their own work.]

### Close

[Question that invites engagement. NOT a lecture. "What's your experience with X?" or "Where do you see this playing out?"]

### Hashtags (3-5, relevant)

#[industry] #[topic] #[framework-name] #[audience] #[action]

### Graphic

[1-2 custom graphics: framework diagram, before/after comparison, or data visualization.]

---

## Adaptation Checklist

- [ ] Written in first person ("I've found..." not "It has been observed...")
- [ ] Evidence-driven (every claim backed by specific example)
- [ ] Never copy-pasted from Substack — rewritten entirely
- [ ] Line breaks every 2-3 sentences
- [ ] Link to newsletter in FIRST COMMENT, not post body
- [ ] 200-400 words total
- [ ] Tone: professional but not corporate-speak
