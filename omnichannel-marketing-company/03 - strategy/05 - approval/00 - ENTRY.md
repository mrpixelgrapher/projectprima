# 05 · Approval

**Purpose:** get the client's yes on one page before any content is produced. Unclear scope blocks the task; it is never a footnote.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `03-strategy/01-positioning.md` to `04-objectives.md`; `01-intake/00-request.md`; `CONTEXT.md`.
- **Writes:** `03-strategy/05-strategy-summary.md` (the send unit) and `client/approval-strategy.md` (the reply, verbatim).
- **Done when:** the reply is logged, and its outcome is APPROVED, or APPROVED WITH CHANGES with the changes applied.

## Procedure

1. **Write the summary.** One page, in the client's vocabulary, with no internal IDs. It has these sections in this order:
   - what you asked for (their words);
   - who we'll write for;
   - what we'll say (the promise and the pillars);
   - where (the hub and the channels, one line of role each);
   - what you'll get (the package from the frame, with counts: pieces × channels);
   - what success means (the objectives);
   - **decisions we need from you** (at most 4: the name, the hub, targets, voice adjustments, claims to prove);
   - what we assume.
2. **Send it,** unless the task is a rehearsal. Set `waiting on` to `client approval`.
3. **Log the reply** verbatim in `client/approval-strategy.md`, with a map from each decision to the client's answer.
4. **Apply the outcome.**

   | Reply | Action |
   |---|---|
   | APPROVED | Record the decisions; go to `06 - gate/` |
   | APPROVED WITH CHANGES | Apply each change to the stage file it affects, and log "changed per client" with the quote. If a change alters the position statement, rerun stages 2–4 |
   | REJECTED, or a new direction | Send the task back to the stage the reply points at, and log the reason |
   | No reply | The gate holds; `waiting on` stays |

## Output template

    # Strategy summary — for <client label>
    ## What you asked for
    ## Who we'll write for
    ## What we'll say
    ## Where
    ## What you'll get
    ## What success means
    ## Decisions we need from you
    1. … (options / our recommendation and why)
    ## What we'll assume unless you tell us otherwise

## Worked example

Gemstone task *(illustrative)*: decision 1: "The name for the gift line: Kora Gifts (our recommendation: short, reads as a gift, and keeps your name) / Stoneline Corporate / your own idea". Decision 2: "Home for articles: Substack, since you have no blog yet. Yes / we'd rather build a blog on our site".

## Self-check

- [ ] Is the summary one page, without jargon or internal IDs?
- [ ] Are there at most 4 decisions, each with options and a stated reason for any recommendation?
- [ ] Is the reply logged verbatim, and its outcome applied?

## Traps

- **Burying the decisions.** If the name choice sits in paragraph four, it won't be answered.
- **Silence read as approval.** No reply means HOLD.
