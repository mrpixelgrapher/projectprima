# Channel roles

**Purpose:** the reference table the channel stage chooses from. It gives each channel's role, who it reaches, its register, the seeds it develops, and how it links back to the hub. Merged from the archived distribution architecture, the platform registry, the working rules and spec 03. Platform limits marked [VERIFY] are checked with query-bank questions 12–14 (`02 - discovery/02 - research/query-bank.md`) before shaping relies on them.

## Flow

    Hub (canonical long-form, owned) ──► every spoke. One way: spokes point back to the hub, never the reverse.

## Channels

| Channel | Role | Reaches | Register | Seeds developed | Link back to hub |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hub (site blog / owned newsletter / Substack) | Canonical long-form; owned list; archive | Direct subscribers, search | 2,000–5,000 words, SEO-structured | the full piece | — |
| LinkedIn | Professional authority | B2B decision-makers, clients, partners | Executive, evidence-led, 200–400 words; first 3 lines are the hook; 3–5 hashtags | core insight, framework, contrarian | First comment in weeks 1–3; in the body for the week-4 recap |
| Twitter/X | Process exposure | People curious about the method | Thread of 5–12 posts; first post under 280 characters | process, contrarian | Final post only |
| Threads | Implications and discussion | People curious about outcomes | One post, ≤ 500 characters [VERIFY], invites replies | implication | In the post |
| Facebook | Broad reach to mature and business-owner readers | Business owners, mature readers, investors | Accessible, educational, 300–600 words | implication, core insight, visual concept | In the post body |
| Instagram | Visual layer | Visual-first audiences | Carousel of 5–10 slides, one seed per slide | visual concept, framework | Link in bio |
| Medium | Discovery republish | Readers searching the topic | A different title, executive-summary angle, 800–1,500 words | core insight, process | Canonical link to the hub [VERIFY] |
| Website page | Conversion layer | Prospects evaluating the client | Executive summary with one call to action | core insight | Links to the full piece on the hub |
| Email | Direct nurture | Existing contacts and leads | One idea, one call to action, short | core insight or implication | Link to the hub |
| Ads (per platform) | Paid reach to a chosen segment | Targeted buyers | Platform ad formats: primary text, headline, description [VERIFY: the platform's current specs] | core insight, contrarian, proof | Landing page on the website or hub |
| Sales collateral | Direct B2B selling | Buyers already in a buying process | One-pager, catalogue, or deck copy | framework, proof | Contact and order details; hub link for depth |

## Seed map

| Seed | Primary channel | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Core insight | LinkedIn | Medium, website, email |
| Process breakdown | Twitter/X | Medium |
| Contrarian angle | LinkedIn, Twitter/X | ads |
| Framework / model | LinkedIn | Instagram, sales collateral |
| Implication | Threads | Facebook, email |
| Visual concept | Facebook | Instagram |

**Seed-split rule:** when two channels use the same seed, each takes a different angle on it.

## Coverage rule

The channels in the plan must reach every buyer profile in the brief. If a profile is reached by no channel, add one, or record why that profile is out of scope.
