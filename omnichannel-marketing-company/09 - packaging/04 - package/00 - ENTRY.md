# 04 · Package

**Purpose:** write the one page the client opens first. It says what they are getting, how to publish it step by step, what is still waiting and why, and what to send back. It is written for someone with no marketing know-how.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `09-packaging/02-kit-index.md`, `03-schedule.md`; the parent's `03-strategy/05-strategy-summary.md`, `04-objectives.md`; `08-shaping/03-channel-index.md` (in each piece).
- **Writes:** `09-packaging/00-package.md`.
- **Done when:** all six sections are written in plain words, and every waiting item is named.

## Procedure

Write these six sections, in this order:

1. **What's inside:** N pieces × M channels; a list of the pieces (title and one line each), and the folder each lives in.
2. **How to publish:** the steps per channel, e.g. "LinkedIn: paste the text from `02-linkedin.md`; add the image; post; paste the link as the first comment." Follow `03-schedule.md`.
3. **What's still waiting, and why:** image briefs with no producer (with an option: hand the briefs in `images/` to a designer), and client-supplied facts (the exact list).
4. **What we assumed:** every assumption still standing from intake, discovery or strategy.
5. **What to send back:** the live URL of every post after publishing, and any results against the objectives on the review date. This feeds `10 - delivery`.
6. **What success looks like:** the objectives in plain words, and when you'll check them together.

## Output template

    # Your content package — <client label>
    ## What's inside
    ## How to publish
    ## What's still waiting, and why
    ## What we assumed
    ## What to send back
    ## What success looks like

## Self-check

- [ ] Could the client publish everything from this page plus the kit alone?
- [ ] Is every waiting item listed, with what to do about it?
- [ ] Is it free of internal IDs and jargon?

## Traps

- **An index that points to files but never says what to do with them.**
