# 02 · Schedule

**Purpose:** turn the approved calendar into a posting schedule that points at each kit file, so whoever posts (the client, or our publishing node) follows one list, day by day.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `24-planning/02-calendar.md`; `26-packaging/01-kit-index.md` and the kit files.
- **Writes:** `26-packaging/02-schedule.md`.
- **Done when:** every calendar post appears once with its kit file, and the rules below hold.

## Procedure

1. **Copy the calendar rows** in date and time order, and add each post's kit file path.
2. **Check the rules** (the calendar should already hold them; this is the last look before posting): no two channels post the same piece on the same day; each channel's count equals its engagement quantity; every test variant has its equal share.
3. **Who posts:** from the engagement: "us" if publishing is included, otherwise "you".
4. **Changes** (a date the client moved at approval, a post held for a client fact): note them in a "Changed from the calendar" list.

## Output template

    # Schedule — <client> · <month>
    | Date | Time | Channel | Kit file | Link placement | Test variant | Who posts |
    ## Changed from the calendar

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* `| 2026-11-10 | 12:30 | Instagram | kit/03-instagram/2026-11-10-P2-real-or-fake.md | bio | T1-A | you |`

- [ ] Does every kit file appear exactly once?
