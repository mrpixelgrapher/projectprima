# 03 · Package

**Purpose:** the one page the client opens first: what they are getting, how to post it step by step, what is still waiting and why, and what to send back. Written for someone with no marketing know-how, in their language.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `26-packaging/01-kit-index.md`, `02-schedule.md`; `22-strategy/03-destination.md`, `05-objectives.md` (month task: `clients/<client>/strategy.md`); `23-performance/05-tracking.md`; `clients/<client>/engagement.md`.
- **Writes:** `26-packaging/03-package.md`.
- **Done when:** all six sections are written in plain words, and every waiting item is named.

## Procedure

Write these six sections, in this order:

1. **What's inside:** the month; N pieces × M channels; each piece's title and one line; the kit folder per channel.
2. **How to post** (if the client posts): the steps per channel, e.g. "Instagram: open `kit/03-instagram/`; post each file on its date; add its images in order; put the link in your bio." Point to the schedule. If we post: "We post on the dates in the schedule; you'll get every live link."
3. **What's still waiting, and why:** each `CLIENT TO SUPPLY` item, where it goes, and by when.
4. **Where everyone ends up:** the destination and its one action, and the one thing to check there (the form works, the catalogue downloads).
5. **What to send back:** the live link of every post you publish; and, on day 20, the numbers from the tracking list (retainer).
6. **What success looks like:** the objectives in plain words, and when we'll look at them together.

## Output template

    # Your content for <month> — <client label>
    ## What's inside
    ## How to post
    ## What's still waiting, and why
    ## Where everyone ends up
    ## What to send back
    ## What success looks like

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* "Where everyone ends up: every post sends people to your catalogue page. Before the 3rd, open it on your phone and request the catalogue yourself, to check it arrives."

## Self-check

- [ ] Could the client post everything from this page plus the kit alone?
- [ ] Is every waiting item listed with what to do?
- [ ] Is it free of internal IDs and jargon?

## Traps

- **An index that points to files but never says what to do with them.**
