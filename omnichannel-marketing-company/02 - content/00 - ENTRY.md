# 02 - content

**Purpose:** make the content the client bought. Research the client's world and the top creators in it, set a strategy with one destination online, design the content to spread and convert, plan each month, write every piece natively for every channel, package it, publish it when that is included, and deliver. Every piece drives traffic to the destination.

A task enters this department only after `01 - commercial` has agreed the scope and been paid its advance, or, on the direct route (no commercials, operator directive I-013), after scope has fixed the deliverables. The engagement (`clients/<client>/engagement.md`) is the scope: nothing is made that it doesn't list.

## Nodes (each node folder's `00 - ENTRY.md` is its instruction)

| # | Node | One job | On route |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01 - discovery/` | Send ARENA the research and top-creator sampling (and the client any questions); turn what returns into the client brief, the creator library and the voiceprint | engagement |
| 2 | `02 - strategy/` | Positioning, messages, the destination, the channel plan and objectives; the client approves | engagement |
| 3 | `03 - performance/` | Design the content to spread and convert: conversion path, virality mechanics, tests, tracking; each month, optimise from results | engagement, month |
| 4 | `04 - planning/` | The month plan and calendar: angles that funnel to the destination, plus standalone pieces; one brief per piece; the client approves | engagement, month |
| 5 | `05 - writing/` | The writing department: research each topic (ARENA), draft, voice, shape for every channel, scripts and storyboards, still prompts (image factory), review and human check | engagement, month |
| 6 | `06 - packaging/` | The paste-ready kit per channel, the schedule, and the client package | engagement, month |
| 7 | `07 - publishing/` | Post on the client's accounts, per the calendar, and log every link | when the engagement includes publishing |
| 8 | `08 - delivery/` | Hand over, record what happened and the hours spent, plan the results, and turn lessons into instruction fixes | engagement, month |

Video: when the engagement includes video, the task goes from `05 - writing/` to `03 - video-factory/` and returns to `06 - packaging/` (`00 - control/01 - law/ROUTES.md`).

## What every node here obeys

- `00 - control/01 - law/OMNICHANNEL_ENFORCEMENT.md`: one source, channel-native variants, every channel in the plan.
- `00 - control/01 - law/PERFECTIONISM_ENFORCEMENT.md`: every artifact a client sees names its crafted surplus.
- `00 - control/01 - law/HANDOFFS.md`: research goes to ARENA, stills to the image factory, questions to the client, all carried by the operator; the task waits for each return.
- No paid ads. The performance is designed into organic content.
