# ledgers

**Purpose:** the content department's memory of every delivered month. Each task appends one row per ledger at `02 - record/` and `04 - lessons/`. These rows are the evidence behind any claim the company makes about what it has delivered and how long it takes. Tier 2: append rows only; never edit a logged row, and add a correcting row instead.

| Ledger | One row per | Written at |
|---|---|---|
| `delivery-ledger.md` | delivered task | 02 - record |
| `lessons-log.md` | lesson | 04 - lessons |

Rows from `rehearsal` tasks carry `rehearsal` in the kind column, and never count as delivery. Client, proposal, invoice and upsell ledgers are in `01 - commercial/ledgers/`.
