# 02 · Messages

**Purpose:** break the position into 3–5 message pillars that pieces can argue one at a time, and fix the brand voice rules the drafters must follow.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `22-strategy/01-positioning.md`, `21-discovery/07-client-brief.md`, `21-discovery/06-voiceprint.md`.
- **Writes:** `22-strategy/02-messages.md`.
- **Done when:** the one-line promise, 3–5 pillars (each with claim, proof, objection and stage), and the voice rules are written.

## Procedure

1. **One-line promise.** Compress the position statement into a line of at most 12 words that a buyer could repeat.
2. **Pillars.** Give each pillar:
   - **claim:** one sentence the buyer should come to believe;
   - **proof:** points from the brief, each cited;
   - **objection answered:** one line from the buyer profile's "fears";
   - **buyer stage:** *aware* (knows the problem), *considering* (comparing options) or *deciding* (ready to choose).

   Cover all three stages across the pillars. A pillar with no proof is cut or rewritten.
3. **Voice rules.**
   - **F1 = EXISTING or THIRD-PARTY:** copy the voiceprint's tell and 5 signature words, and add the buyer's words to prefer or avoid.
   - **F1 = NEW:** start from the founder's voiceprint. Choose what to keep, adjust or drop for the new brand, with a reason tied to the buyer. The client approves this at `06 - approval/`.
   - **Voice modelled on creators:** keep the voiceprint's rhythm and devices; state the client's own stance and claims, never the creator's.
4. **Words to use / never use.** Up to 10 each. Take them from the buyer's words (brief) and the claims rules (Limits).

## Output template

    # Messages — <task id>
    Promise: …
    ## Pillars
    | # | Claim | Proof (cited) | Objection answered | Buyer stage |
    ## Voice rules

## Worked example

Gemstone task *(illustrative)*: Promise: "Real gemstones, certified, on time for every festival." Pillar 2: claim "Every stone can be verified" · proof: lab report with each piece [client] · objection: "gemstone gifts might be fake" · stage: considering.

## Self-check

- [ ] Is the promise at most 12 words?
- [ ] Does every pillar have cited proof and a named objection?
- [ ] Do the pillars cover aware, considering and deciding?
- [ ] Are the voice rules traceable to the voiceprint?

## Traps

- **Topics as pillars.** "Sustainability" is a topic; "every stone is traceable to its mine" is a claim.
- **A voice invented from scratch for a new brand.** Start from the founder's real voice.
