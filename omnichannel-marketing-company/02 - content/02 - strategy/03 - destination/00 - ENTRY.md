# 03 · Destination

**Purpose:** choose the one place online where every piece sends its readers, decide what lives there and the one action it asks for. Every channel's call to action points here, and performance measures the job here.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `21-discovery/07-client-brief.md` (slots GOAL, CHANNELS, OFFER; buyer profiles); `21-discovery/05-creator-library.md` (funnel mechanics); `22-strategy/01-positioning.md`, `02-messages.md`; `clients/<client>/engagement.md` (is a destination build bought?).
- **Writes:** `22-strategy/03-destination.md`.
- **Done when:** the destination, its one action, what lives there, the lead magnet (or "none" and why), and who builds it are stated, each with its rule or evidence.

## Procedure

1. **Choose the destination** by the first row that applies:

   | Situation (from the brief) | Destination |
   |---|---|
   | The client owns a newsletter with subscribers | That newsletter |
   | The client owns a site where a visitor can act (order, enquire, subscribe) | A dedicated page on that site |
   | GOAL is orders, enquiries or meetings, and neither exists | A landing page with one action (built by us: setup deliverable "destination build") |
   | GOAL is audience, authority or reach, and neither exists | A newsletter (Substack: free, owned list, archive) |

   If the engagement did not buy a destination build and one is needed, it is a decision for the client at approval (add-on or they build it).
2. **One action.** The single thing a visitor does there: subscribe, enquire, order, book. It serves the GOAL's metric. Never two.
3. **What lives there:** the headline (the promise from messages), 3 proof points (from the pillars), the action, and, for a landing page, the offer in one paragraph. For a newsletter: the welcome issue and what subscribers get each week.
4. **Lead magnet.** Use one if the creator library shows a lead-magnet pattern in this niche (2+ creators); it is the reason to act now (a guide, a checklist, a catalogue). Otherwise "none", with that reason.
5. **Link path.** Every channel's link to the destination carries a tracking tag, set at `02 - content/03 - performance/05 - tracking/`. Name the address, or "to be created" when we build it.

## Worked example

Gemstone task *(illustrative)*: GOAL "10 company orders before diwali"; no site, no newsletter → a landing page with one action, **"Request the corporate catalogue"**; lead magnet: the festival gifting catalogue PDF (3 gifting creators end carousels with a catalogue download [L-022, L-026, L-029]); built by us (setup deliverable D-S4).

## Self-check

- [ ] Was the destination picked by the first matching row?
- [ ] Is there exactly one action, tied to an objective?
- [ ] Is the lead magnet backed by the creator library, or "none" with a reason?

## Traps

- **A social profile as the destination.** A platform can change its reach overnight; the destination is owned.
- **Two actions.** "Subscribe or book a call" halves both. One action.
