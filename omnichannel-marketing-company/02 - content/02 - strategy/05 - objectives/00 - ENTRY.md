# 05 · Objectives

**Purpose:** say what success means in numbers the client agrees to, and how and when it will be read. The monthly review (`01 - commercial/05 - month-review/`) reads results against these.

## Contract

- **Reads:** slot GOAL and slot LIMITS in `21-discovery/07-client-brief.md`; `22-strategy/03-destination.md`; `22-strategy/04-channel-plan.md`.
- **Writes:** `22-strategy/05-objectives.md`.
- **Done when:** 1–3 objectives, each with a metric, baseline, target, source, reader and review date.

## Procedure

1. **Objectives.** Write 1–3, taken from GOAL. Each names **one** metric the client can see, measured at the destination where possible: its one action (enquiries, orders, sign-ups), then visits.
2. **Baseline.** Use the client's current number [client]. If they don't know it, the first objective is *"establish the baseline"*: measure for the first 30 days, and set the target after that.
3. **Target.** Use only a number the client stated or approves. Never invent one. An unapproved target becomes an approval item in `06 - approval/`.
4. **Reading.** Name where the number is read (inbox, newsletter dashboard, site analytics, order book), who reads it, and when: every month on day 20 for a retainer (the month review), or 30 days after the last publication for a one-off.
5. **Rule.** Each objective states its on-track rule, e.g. "on track if the month's enquiries ≥ target ÷ months". The month review applies it.
6. **Tie to channels.** Each objective names the channels in the plan expected to move it.

## Output template

    # Objectives — <task id>
    | # | Objective | Metric | Baseline | Target | On-track rule | Read where / by whom | Review | Channels |

## Worked example

Gemstone task *(illustrative)*: O1: "first corporate orders" · metric: enquiries from HR leads · baseline: 0 (a new line) · target: 10 qualified enquiries before Diwali (the client's own goal) · on-track rule: ≥ 5 by the first month review · read where: the landing page's enquiry form, by the founder · review: day 20 each month · channels: Instagram, LinkedIn.

## Self-check

- [ ] Does every objective have a single, visible metric?
- [ ] Does every baseline and target come from the client, or is it marked as an approval item?
- [ ] Is there a review date and an on-track rule?

## Traps

- **Vanity metrics.** Impressions are not a business objective unless the client's GOAL is awareness.
- **Invented targets.** A number the client never agreed to becomes a broken promise at delivery.
