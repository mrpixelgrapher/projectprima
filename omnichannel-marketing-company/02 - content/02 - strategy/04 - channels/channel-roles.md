# Channel roles

**Purpose:** the reference table the channel stage reads. It gives each channel's role, who it reaches, its register, the seeds it develops, and how it links to the destination. Merged from the archived distribution architecture, the platform registry, the working rules and spec 03. Platform limits marked [VERIFY] are checked through ARENA with query-bank questions 12–14 (`02 - content/01 - discovery/01 - requests/query-bank.md`) before writing relies on them.

## Flow

    Core long-form (research-backed) ──► a native variant on every channel in the plan ──► every variant sends readers to the destination.
    One way: variants point to the long-form home and the destination, never the reverse.

## Channels

| Channel | Role | Reaches | Register | Seeds developed | Link to the destination |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Substack / owned newsletter | Canonical long-form and owned list; often the destination | Direct subscribers, search | 1,500–4,000 words, one idea per issue | the full piece | — (it is the home, or links to the destination) |
| Website blog | Canonical long-form on the client's own site; search | Search, prospects | 1,500–4,000 words, SEO-structured | the full piece | The destination's action at the end |
| Landing page | The destination: one offer, one action | Everyone the channels send | Headline, 3 proof points, the offer, the action | core insight, proof | — (it is the destination) |
| LinkedIn | Professional authority | B2B decision-makers, clients, partners | Executive, evidence-led, 200–400 words; first 3 lines are the hook; 3–5 hashtags | core insight, framework, contrarian | First comment (or in the body, if the creator patterns show that performs in this niche) |
| Twitter/X | Process exposure | People curious about the method | Thread of 5–12 posts; first post under 280 characters | process, contrarian | Final post only |
| Threads | Implications and discussion | People curious about outcomes | One post, ≤ 500 characters [VERIFY], invites replies | implication | In the post |
| Facebook | Broad reach to mature and business-owner readers | Business owners, mature readers, investors | Accessible, educational, 300–600 words | implication, core insight, visual concept | In the post body |
| Instagram | Visual layer | Visual-first audiences | Carousel of 5–10 slides, one seed per slide | visual concept, framework | Link in bio |
| Medium | Discovery republish | Readers searching the topic | A different title, executive-summary angle, 800–1,500 words | core insight, process | Canonical link to the long-form home [VERIFY]; the destination's action at the end |
| Newsletter issue / email | Direct nurture of the owned list | Subscribers, existing contacts | One idea, one call to action, short | core insight or implication | The destination's action |
| Pinterest | Search-led visual discovery | Planners and researchers | Pin title and description; tall image | visual concept, framework | The pin links to the destination |

## Seed map

| Seed | Primary channel | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Core insight | LinkedIn | Medium, website, email |
| Process breakdown | Twitter/X | Medium |
| Contrarian angle | LinkedIn, Twitter/X | Threads |
| Framework / model | LinkedIn | Instagram, Pinterest |
| Implication | Threads | Facebook, email |
| Visual concept | Instagram | Facebook, Pinterest |

**Seed-split rule:** when two channels use the same seed, each takes a different angle on it.

## Coverage rule

The channels in the plan must reach every buyer profile in the brief. If a profile is reached by no channel, add one, or record why that profile is out of scope.

Paid ads and sales collateral are not offered (design lock, S003): the company makes organic content engineered to convert. Their v1 rows are in `99 - archive/2026-09-28-pipeline-v1/03 - strategy/03 - channels/channel-roles.md`.
