# 04 · Channels

**Purpose:** give every channel the client bought a job: its role, the buyer it reaches, its focus, its cadence, and how it moves readers to the destination. Writing produces a variant for exactly these channels.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `clients/<client>/engagement.md` (the channels and quantities bought); `21-discovery/07-client-brief.md` (buyer profiles: "where they pay attention"; slots EXECUTION, LANGUAGE); `21-discovery/05-creator-library.md`; `22-strategy/03-destination.md`; `channel-roles.md` in this folder.
- **Writes:** `22-strategy/04-channel-plan.md`.
- **Done when:** every channel in the engagement has a row with all columns filled; every channel in `channel-roles.md` not bought is listed with its reason; every buyer profile is reached by at least one channel.

## Procedure

1. **The long-form home.** The core long-form pieces live on the destination when it can hold them (a newsletter or a site blog). If the destination is a landing page, they live on the long-form channel the client bought (blog, Substack, Medium), each ending with the destination's action.
2. **One row per channel bought.** Take its role, register and seeds from `channel-roles.md`; its cadence from the engagement quantities; its language from LANGUAGE; who posts from EXECUTION (us if the engagement includes publishing); its link path to the destination from the destination file.
3. **Focus for this client:** one line per channel: which pillar leads there and which buyer profile it reaches, citing the brief. A channel the brief shows the buyer does not use still stays (the client bought it), but its focus says so and names what it is for.
4. **Creator patterns:** name, per channel, the 2–3 patterns from the creator library the content will use first.
5. **Not bought:** every other channel in `channel-roles.md`: "not in the engagement; available as an add-on" (with the add-on price from the engagement), or "not offered" (paid ads).
6. **Coverage:** every buyer profile must be reached by at least one channel. If one isn't, say so; it is a decision for the client at approval.

## Output template

    Long-form home: … (rule: …) · Destination: … · One action: …
    ## Channels in the plan
    | Channel | Role | Buyer | Focus for this client | Seeds | Patterns to use first | Cadence | Language | Posts | Link to destination |
    ## Not in the plan
    | Channel | Reason |
    ## Coverage
    | Buyer profile | Reached by |

## Worked example

Gemstone task *(illustrative)*: Instagram · role: visual layer · buyer: HR lead (A) · focus: pillar 2 "every stone can be verified", as carousels · patterns: "What <budget> buys you" cover; catalogue link on the last slide · 8 a month · English · client posts · link: link in bio → landing page.

## Self-check

- [ ] Does every channel in the engagement have a complete row?
- [ ] Does every row send readers to the destination?
- [ ] Is every buyer profile reached?

## Traps

- **Planning channels nobody bought.** The engagement is the scope; extra channels are add-ons.
- **A channel with no path to the destination.** Content that sends no one anywhere doesn't serve the goal.
