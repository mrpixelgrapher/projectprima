# 02 · Conversion

**Purpose:** design the path a reader takes from first sight of a post to the destination's one action, stage by stage, and name the principle that moves them at each step. Planning gives every piece a job on this path; writing applies the principles.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `22-strategy/02-messages.md` (pillars, buyer stages, objections), `22-strategy/03-destination.md`, `21-discovery/07-client-brief.md` (buyer profiles, proof), `21-discovery/05-creator-library.md` (funnel mechanics); `23-performance/01-results.md`.
- **Writes:** `23-performance/02-conversion.md`.
- **Done when:** each stage of the path (stop · engage · trust · act) has its job, the principles used with their true basis, the pieces' call to action, and the objection it answers; the weakest link is named on a month route.

## Procedure

1. **Map the path** in four stages, each tied to the pillars' buyer stages:

   | Stage | The reader… | Buyer stage |
   |---|---|---|
   | Stop | stops scrolling | aware |
   | Engage | reads or watches to the end, saves or shares | aware → considering |
   | Trust | believes the claim is true for them | considering |
   | Act | goes to the destination and does the one action | deciding |

2. **Choose the principles for each stage** from this table. Use a principle only when its "true basis" exists in the brief; write the basis next to it.

   | Principle | What it does | Stage | True basis required |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Pattern interrupt | Breaks the expected feed pattern | Stop | — |
   | Curiosity gap | Opens a question the post then answers | Stop, Engage | The post must answer it |
   | Specificity | Exact numbers and names are believed over round claims | Stop, Trust | A sourced number |
   | Loss framing | People weigh losses more than equal gains | Stop, Act | A real cost of the status quo, sourced |
   | Social proof | People follow what similar people do | Trust | Real clients, numbers or reviews in hand |
   | Authority | Credentials shorten the trust decision | Trust | Proof in hand (certificate, record) |
   | Reciprocity | A useful gift invites a return | Act | The lead magnet exists |
   | Commitment ladder | A small yes makes the next yes easier | Engage → Act | A low-effort first step (save, reply, download) |
   | Cognitive ease | Easy-to-read claims feel truer | all | One idea per post; plain words |
   | Peak-end | The strongest moment and the ending are remembered | Engage | — |
   | Urgency | A deadline prompts action now | Act | A real deadline (a festival date, a real cut-off) |

3. **Month route: fix the weakest link.** From the results, the stage where readers drop most (e.g. many views, few destination visits → Act is weak). Change that stage's principles or call to action first; leave the rest.
4. **Calls to action:** one per stage, in the client's language, each pointing to the next step and, at Act, to the destination's one action.
5. **Objections:** assign each buyer objection from the messages to the stage where it must be answered.

## Output template

    # Conversion path — <task id>
    | Stage | Job | Principles (true basis) | Call to action | Objection answered |
    Weakest link (month route): … → change: …

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* Trust · job: "prove the stones are real" · principles: authority (every piece ships with its lab report [client]), specificity ("2.1-carat Ceylon blue, unheated") · CTA: "See a real lab report in the catalogue" · objection: "gemstone gifts might be fake".

## Self-check

- [ ] Does every principle used have its true basis written next to it?
- [ ] Does every stage have one call to action, and does Act point to the destination's one action?
- [ ] On a month route, is the weakest link named from the numbers?

## Traps

- **Manufactured urgency.** "Only 3 left" when there are 300 is a lie. No real deadline, no urgency.
- **Principles without a path.** A list of tactics is not a design; each must serve a stage.
