# 03 · Virality

**Purpose:** for each channel in the plan, decide what will make a piece get shared, saved and commented on, from the patterns the creator library shows actually spread in this niche.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `21-discovery/05-creator-library.md` (or `clients/<client>/creators.md`); `22-strategy/04-channel-plan.md`; `23-performance/01-results.md`.
- **Writes:** `23-performance/03-virality.md`.
- **Done when:** each channel has its share trigger, save trigger and comment trigger, the hook forms to use first, the format and length, and the cadence and timing, each citing the creator library or a result.

## Procedure

1. **For each channel,** choose from the library's patterns (2+ creators, 2+ posts) and from last month's winners:

   | Trigger | Ask | Use when the library shows |
   |---|---|---|
   | Share | Would a reader send this to someone to look smart, helpful or understood? | Posts in the niche that are shared carry a useful number, a clear framework, or a strong shared opinion |
   | Save | Is this worth coming back to? | Checklists, templates, "what X buys you" lists, step-by-steps |
   | Comment | Does it invite a reply the reader wants to give? | Questions with a stake, a choice between two options, a mild contrarian claim |

2. **Hooks to use first:** 2–3 hook forms from the library per channel, with the pattern's evidence. Never a saturated angle.
3. **Format and length:** the library's best-performing structure per channel (slides in a carousel, posts in a thread, words in a post).
4. **Cadence and timing:** from the library's observed cadence, within the engagement's quantity.
5. **Month route:** replace any trigger or hook that lost its test; keep winners.

## Output template

    # Virality — <task id>
    | Channel | Share trigger | Save trigger | Comment trigger | Hooks first | Format · length | Cadence · timing | Evidence |

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* Instagram: save trigger "What ₹5,000 buys you: 5 gifts HR teams re-order" (checklist-style carousel [L-021, L-024]); comment trigger "Which would your team pick: A or B?"; format: 7-slide carousel; Tue/Thu 12:30.

## Self-check

- [ ] Does every trigger cite the library or a result?
- [ ] Are saturated angles excluded?

## Traps

- **Virality over the goal.** A post that spreads but sends no one to the destination misses the job. Every trigger sits inside the conversion path.
