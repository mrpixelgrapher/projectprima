# 03 - performance

Status: BUILT (session S003, 2026-09-28)

**Purpose:** engineer the organic content to perform: to spread on its own and to convert readers at the destination, the way good ad creative does, without paid ads. The node designs the conversion path on neuromarketing principles, the virality mechanics per channel from what the creator library shows spreads, the tests that tell us what works, and the tracking that measures it. Every retainer month it runs again and optimises from the results.

**Use when:** a task arrives from `02 - strategy` (engagement route) or from `01 - commercial/06 - billing-advance` (month route).

## The line this node holds

Persuasion here is honest: every principle is applied to true claims, real deadlines and proof in hand. A tactic that would need a false statement (fake scarcity, invented numbers, testimonials we don't have) is never used, whatever it would do for conversion.
