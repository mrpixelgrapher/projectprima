# 04 · Tests

**Purpose:** decide what this month tests, so each month the design learns one or two things for certain instead of guessing.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `23-performance/01-results.md`, `02-conversion.md`, `03-virality.md`; `clients/<client>/engagement.md` (quantities).
- **Writes:** `23-performance/04-tests.md`.
- **Done when:** 1–2 tests are defined, each with one variable, its variants, the channel, the pieces it uses, the metric, the threshold and the reading date.

## Procedure

1. **Pick at most 2 tests.** Priority: the weakest link from the conversion stage; then an inconclusive test extended from last month; then the biggest open question in virality.
2. **One variable per test:** the hook form, the format, the call to action or its placement, the posting time. Everything else is held the same.
3. **Enough posts:** each variant needs at least 4 posts in the month on that channel; if the engagement's quantity can't give that, test on a channel that can, or test one variable over two months.
4. **Metric and threshold:** one metric from tracking (saves, shares, destination visits from that channel); a variant wins when it beats the other by at least 20%. Below that, the test is inconclusive.

## Output template

    # Tests — <task id> · <month>
    | Test | Variable | Variant A | Variant B | Channel | Posts each | Metric | Threshold | Read on |

## Traps

- **Testing everything.** Five variables at once teaches nothing.
