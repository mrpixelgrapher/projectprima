# 03 · Creators

**Purpose:** turn the top-creator sampling into a library of patterns per channel (what hooks, structures, angles, funnel mechanics, cadence and visuals work in this niche now), so performance, planning and writing build on what demonstrably spreads and converts, never on taste.

## Contract

- **Reads:** the creator-sampling sections of `21-discovery/arena-dossier-1/`; `21-discovery/03-sources.md`.
- **Writes:** `21-discovery/05-creator-library.md`.
- **Done when:** every channel in the engagement has its creators listed and a pattern card for each of the six dimensions, each pattern backed by at least 2 sampled posts (by link), and a "saturated" list.

## Procedure

1. **List the creators** per channel: name or handle, link, audience size if given, why they were sampled (the dossier's reason), each logged as a source (L-ID).
2. **For each channel, write six pattern cards,** one per dimension. A pattern counts only if it appears in **at least 2 posts from at least 2 different creators**; otherwise it goes under "seen once".

   | Dimension | A pattern card states |
   |---|---|
   | Hooks | The hook's form (e.g. "a number + a surprising cost"), 2+ example links, why it stops the scroll |
   | Structures and formats | The post shape (thread of 8, carousel of 7 slides, 900-word essay…), its parts in order |
   | Angles | The angle, whether it performs or looks saturated (the same angle by 3+ creators in the sample → saturated) |
   | Funnel mechanics | How they move readers off-platform: the call to action, lead magnet, where the link sits, cross-posting |
   | Cadence and timing | Posts per week, days and times, as observed |
   | Visual style | Cover and slide style, image type, text on images, colour and type conventions |

3. **Write "openings for us":** angles no sampled creator covers that the client's brief could own (these feed strategy's positioning).
4. **Never copy text.** Quote at most one short phrase per post as evidence, with its link. Our content uses the pattern, never the words.

## Output template

    # Creator library — <client> · <date> · from <task id>
    ## <Channel>
    ### Creators
    | Creator | Link | Audience | Why sampled | Source |
    ### Patterns
    | Dimension | Pattern | Evidence (post links) | Creators |
    ### Saturated angles
    ### Seen once
    ## Openings for us

## Worked example

Instagram, gifting niche *(illustrative)*:

| Dimension | Pattern | Evidence | Creators |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hooks | "What <budget> buys you" cover slide with the price in the headline | [L-021], [L-024] | 2 |
| Funnel mechanics | Last slide: "Full catalogue — link in bio", with a lead-magnet PDF | [L-022], [L-026], [L-029] | 3 |

## Self-check

- [ ] Does every pattern have 2+ posts from 2+ creators?
- [ ] Is every creator logged as a source?
- [ ] Is there no reused creator text beyond one short evidence phrase?

## Traps

- **One viral post as a pattern.** Once is an anecdote; it goes under "seen once".
- **Copying a great line.** The library holds patterns. A creator's sentence in our content is plagiarism.
