# 05 · Brief

**Purpose:** compile the one file strategy works from: every slot, the buyer profiles, the market map, what the client can prove, and what is still open. Every line cites its evidence.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `21-discovery/02-slot-board.md`, `03-sources.md`, `04-findings.md`, `05-creator-library.md` (openings), `06-voiceprint.md`; `CONTEXT.md`.
- **Writes:** `21-discovery/07-client-brief.md`.
- **Done when:** all six sections of the template are filled, every line carries a citation, and the open-items list holds nothing needed at intake or discovery.

## Procedure

1. **Slots.** Copy the final slot board: 13 rows, each with status, value and evidence.
2. **Buyer profiles.** Write one profile per distinct buyer the BUYER slot names, at most 3. Each profile is a table with these rows: who (role, organisation type and size, place) · their situation · what they want · what they fear, or object to · how they buy (who decides, approves and pays; lead time) · where they pay attention · the words they use. Every row cites a CQ answer or an L-ID. A row with no evidence says "unknown → RQn" or "unknown → CQn". Never fill it by guessing.
3. **Market map.** One row per alternative the buyer considers: competitors, doing nothing, doing it in-house. Give each: how it presents itself (a quote), its price norm if found, and what it leaves unmet. Cite L-IDs. Add the creator library's "openings for us" as the last rows: unmet angles the client could own.
4. **Proof.** Two lists. What the client claims [client]. What buyers expect to see [src: L-…]. Mark each client claim "document in hand" or "asserted only".
5. **Limits.** Budget, timeline, geography, and claims or legal rules, each cited.
6. **Open items.** Everything still not Known: the route (CQ / RQ / AS) and the node that needs it. Nothing here may be needed at intake or discovery.

## Self-check

- [ ] Does every line carry [client…], [src: L-…], [assumed] or an explicit "unknown → route"?
- [ ] Are there no more buyer profiles than the BUYER slot supports?
- [ ] Is every client claim marked in hand or asserted?
- [ ] Does the open-items list contain nothing needed at intake or discovery?

## Traps

- **Invented demographics.** Ages, incomes and hobbies that no source gave.
- **Client claims written as verified.** "Certified stones" with no document in hand is *asserted*.
- **One vague buyer.** "Businesses" is not a profile. Split by who decides, or ask.
