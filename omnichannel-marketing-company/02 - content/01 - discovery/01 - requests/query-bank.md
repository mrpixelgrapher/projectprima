# Query bank

**Purpose:** research questions that almost every founder-led or small-business client needs, ready to copy into an ARENA request as RQs (see this folder's `00 - ENTRY.md`, step 2). Replace `<buyer>`, `<category>` and `<market>` with values from the slot board. Merged from the four lanes of the archived dossier D-001.

## Buyer lane (fills BUYER)

1. How many marketing channels does `<buyer>` typically run, and who runs them (founder, junior hire, freelancer, agency)? Capture survey figures with sample size and year.
2. In `<buyer>`'s own words, what does fragmented or inconsistent marketing cost them (message, attribution, time, spend)? Keep first-person accounts separate from survey data.
3. How does `<buyer>` judge whether marketing works? Which reports do they read, and which do they ignore?
4. For company buyers: who inside the company chooses, who approves, and who pays for `<category>`? What lead times and order sizes are typical?

## Buying-behaviour lane (fills BUYER, PROOF)

5. How does `<buyer>` hire help for `<category>` (agency, freelancer, specialist, in-house)? What are the reported reasons such engagements end?
6. What proof most increases `<buyer>`'s trust in a provider of `<category>`: case studies, referrals, certifications, a trial, guarantees?
7. Price norms only, as context for the client's own pricing: the typical range for `<category>` in `<market>`, with source and region. Never sets our price.

## Alternatives lane (fills MARKET)

8. Which providers compete for `<buyer>`'s budget in `<category>` in `<market>`, and how do they describe their offer? Collect 5+ positioning statements verbatim, with URLs.
9. Which claims in `<category>` do buyers find credible, and which read as jargon or hype? Look at reviews, forums and practitioner commentary.

## Rules lane (fills LIMITS, PROOF)

10. What disclosure, certification or claims rules apply to selling `<category>` in `<market>`? Use official or regulator sources first.
11. For company gifts or hospitality: do companies in `<market>` limit gift value by policy or tax rule? If so, what ranges?

## Channel-facts lane (feeds `02 - content/02 - strategy` and `02 - content/05 - writing`; include it whenever a channel is in the engagement)

12. The current post length limit and recommended image sizes for each channel in the plan, from platform documentation.
13. Does the platform reduce the reach of posts with external links in the body? Log each claim with its evidence type (platform statement, controlled test, anecdote), and surface contradictions.
14. Does Medium (or the planned republishing platform) support canonical links to an original on another domain, and how are they set?
