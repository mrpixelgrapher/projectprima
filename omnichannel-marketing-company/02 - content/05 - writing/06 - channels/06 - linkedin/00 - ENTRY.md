# LinkedIn post

**Purpose:** a native LinkedIn post that gives professional decision-makers one evidence-backed idea from the piece, and sends the interested ones to the destination.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `25-writing/<P>/08-seeds.md` (the LinkedIn assignment); the piece brief (`24-planning/briefs/<P>.md`) (buyer, voice); `template.md` in this folder.
- **Writes:** `25-writing/<P>/channels/linkedin.md`.
- **Done when:** the post meets every rule below, and its metadata block is filled.

## Procedure

1. **Pick the post type from the seed:**

   | Seed | Post type |
   |---|---|
   | core insight | thought leadership |
   | framework | framework-driven educational |
   | contrarian | contrarian insight |
   | process, with a real case in the article | case-study breakdown |
   | implication, when the voice is personal | founder lesson |

2. **The first 3 lines are the hook,** because they show before "see more". Line 1 is a claim or result in at most 15 words; line 2 says why it matters; line 3 makes the reader want to click "see more".
3. **Body.** Evidence (a particular from the seed's development), then the framework or mechanism, then how the reader applies it. Break lines every 2–3 sentences. Write in the first person when the voiceprint uses it.
4. **Close with one question** that invites the reader's own experience. No lecture.
5. **Length:** 200–400 words. **Hashtags:** 3–5, relevant.
6. **The link.** The tagged link from the calendar goes where the channel plan says: in the **first comment** (written as the comment text) or in the body. For a recap post (the calendar's last seed for the piece), in the body.
7. **Image slot `asset-1`:** a framework diagram, a before/after, or a data visual. Describe it for `08 - visuals/` (it becomes an image-factory prompt).

## Output template

Use `template.md`, headed by the metadata block from `06 - channels/00 - ENTRY.md`. Add a line `First comment: <text + tagged link>` when the link goes there.

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* Hook: "Most gemstone gifts can't be verified by the people who receive them. / That's why they end up in a drawer. / Here's the 60-second check we now include with every order ↓"

## Self-check

- [ ] Do the first 3 lines work alone as a hook?
- [ ] Is it 200–400 words, with line breaks every 2–3 sentences and 3–5 hashtags?
- [ ] Is the tagged link where the channel plan says?
- [ ] Is there an `asset-1` slot description, and a closing question?
- [ ] Would a native LinkedIn user find it natural, not a repost?

## Traps

- **An article excerpt with line breaks.** Rewrite it from the seed.
- **A link in the body when the channel plan says first comment.**
- **Corporate voice.** It must sound like the voiceprint, not like a press release.
