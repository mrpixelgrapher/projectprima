# Medium republish

**Purpose:** a condensed, executive-summary version for readers searching the topic on Medium, with a canonical link to the long-form home and the destination's call to action at the end.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `25-writing/<P>/08-seeds.md` (core insight, process); `25-writing/<P>/06-article.md`; the piece brief (`24-planning/briefs/<P>.md`) (voice).
- **Writes:** `25-writing/<P>/channels/medium.md`.
- **Done when:** the version meets every rule below.

## Procedure

1. **A different title from the long-form version,** framed as an executive summary ("What HR should know about…").
2. **Length:** 800–1,500 words, rewritten from the seeds and the article. Not a cut-down copy.
3. **Structure.** The core insight up front, then the process in brief, then one example, then a pointer to the full piece and the destination's call to action (tagged link).
4. **Canonical link to the long-form home.** Record in the metadata how it is set on the platform [VERIFY: current Medium method, query-bank Q14].
5. **Image slot:** reuse the long-form version's `featured` still. Note it in the metadata.

## Output template

The metadata block (add `Canonical: <long-form URL placeholder>`), the title, then the body.

## Self-check

- [ ] Is the title different from the long-form version's, and is the length 800–1,500 words?
- [ ] Is the canonical link recorded?
- [ ] Is no paragraph copied from the article?

## Traps

- **Posting the article again.** That is duplicate content that competes with the long-form home.
