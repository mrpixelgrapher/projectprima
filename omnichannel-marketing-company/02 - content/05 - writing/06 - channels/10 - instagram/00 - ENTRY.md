# Instagram carousel

**Purpose:** a visual-first carousel in which each slide carries one idea, so the piece's framework or visual concept can be understood by swiping.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `25-writing/<P>/08-seeds.md` (visual concept, framework); the piece brief (`24-planning/briefs/<P>.md`) (voice).
- **Writes:** `25-writing/<P>/channels/instagram.md`.
- **Done when:** there are 5–10 slides, each with its text and image slot, plus a caption.

## Procedure

1. **Slide 1 is the hook:** the core insight as a short line.
2. **Slides 2 to N−1:** one idea per slide, in order, usually the framework's steps. Each slide's on-image text is one short sentence.
3. **The last slide** says where the full piece is ("link in bio").
4. **Each slide is an image slot,** `carousel-1` … `carousel-N`, described for `08 - visuals/` with its exact on-image text.
5. **The caption** expands the idea in 2–4 short paragraphs, in the client's voice, and ends with the "link in bio" line.

    Caption: …

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* Slide 1: "Real gemstone or a nice story?" · Slide 2: "Step 1: find the lab name" · Slide 3: "Step 2: match the report number" · …

## Self-check

- [ ] Are there 5–10 slides, one idea each, with every slide's text written?
- [ ] Does every slide have an image slot?
- [ ] Does the caption end with the bio-link line?

## Traps

- **Text walls on slides.** If a slide needs a paragraph, split it.
