# Pinterest

**Purpose:** a pin that people planning or researching the topic find by search, save for later, and click through to the destination.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `25-writing/<P>/08-seeds.md` (visual concept, framework); the piece brief (keyword, voice, tagged link); `23-performance/03-virality.md` (Pinterest row, if any).
- **Writes:** `25-writing/<P>/channels/pinterest.md`.
- **Done when:** the pin has a title, a description, the tagged link and one image slot, following the rules below.

## Procedure

1. **The image does the work:** a tall image (`pin-1`) with a short headline on it that names the benefit ("5 festival gifts HR teams re-order"). Describe it for `08 - visuals/` [VERIFY: current pin size, query-bank Q12].
2. **Title:** the keyword plus the benefit, ≤ 100 characters.
3. **Description:** 2–3 sentences, the keyword and 1–2 related phrases natural in the text, and what the reader gets by clicking.
4. **Link:** the tagged link to the destination (or the full piece, per the channel plan).
5. **Board:** name the board it belongs to (one per topic).

## Output template

The metadata block, then `Title:`, `Description:`, `Board:`, `Link:`, and the `pin-1` slot description.

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* Title: "Corporate festival gifts HR teams actually keep — real gemstone desk pieces" · Board: "Corporate gifting ideas".

## Self-check

- [ ] Is the headline on the image a benefit, and the keyword in the title?

## Traps

- **A social post pasted as a pin.** Pinterest is search: write for what people type.
