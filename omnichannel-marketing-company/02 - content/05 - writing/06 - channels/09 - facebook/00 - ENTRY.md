# Facebook post

**Purpose:** an accessible, educational post that reaches business owners and mature readers, who are less present on the other channels, and sends them to the destination.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `25-writing/<P>/08-seeds.md` (implication, core insight or visual concept); the piece brief (`24-planning/briefs/<P>.md`) (buyer, voice); the piece brief's keyword.
- **Writes:** `25-writing/<P>/channels/facebook.md`.
- **Done when:** the post meets every rule below.

## Procedure

1. **Choose the persona.** Map the brief's buyer to one of: `business-owner`, `mature-reader`, `approaching-retirement-professional`, `investor`, `high-income-segment`. Record it in the metadata.
2. **Frame it to teach.** Open with what the reader will learn. Use plain words, and explain any term.
3. **Length:** 300–600 words.
4. **Keywords.** Use the piece's keyword and 1–3 related phrases naturally.
5. **Put the tagged link in the body.**
6. **Image slot `asset-1`:** one strong, storytelling image of high production value. Describe it for `08 - visuals/` (it becomes an image-factory prompt).
7. **Keep the brand voice.** Adapt to the persona; don't become a different brand.

## Output template

The metadata block (add `Persona: …`, `Keywords: …`), then the post text.

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* Persona: business-owner. Opening: "If you've ever sent a client a gift you couldn't vouch for, this is for you: a one-minute way to know a gemstone is real before it leaves your office."

## Self-check

- [ ] Is a persona declared, and does the framing fit it?
- [ ] Is it 300–600 words, using 2–4 keywords naturally?
- [ ] Is the tagged link in the body, and the `asset-1` slot described?

## Traps

- **LinkedIn tone on Facebook.** Executive shorthand loses this reader.
- **Condescension.** These readers are experienced; teach, don't lecture.
