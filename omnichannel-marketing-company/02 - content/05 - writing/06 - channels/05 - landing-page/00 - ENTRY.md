# Landing page

**Purpose:** the destination itself: one page, one offer, one action. Every channel sends readers here, so this page decides whether the month's traffic becomes the client's result.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `22-strategy/03-destination.md` (the one action, what lives there, the lead magnet); `22-strategy/02-messages.md` (promise, pillars, proof); `23-performance/02-conversion.md` (the Act stage: principles and objections); the piece brief when a piece asks for a page update; `21-discovery/05-creator-library.md` (funnel mechanics).
- **Writes:** `25-writing/<P>/channels/landing-page.md` (a month's update), or, for the setup deliverable "destination build", `25-writing/landing-page.md` (the full page).
- **Done when:** the page has every section below, one action, and every claim on it is backed by proof in hand.

## Procedure

1. **Which job?** Engagement route with "destination build" in the engagement → write the full page once. Month route, or a piece whose brief asks for it → write only the section that changes (a new headline test, a new proof block, the month's lead magnet).
2. **Sections, in order:** headline (the promise, ≤ 12 words) · sub-headline (who it's for and what they get) · 3 proof points (pillars with proof in hand) · the offer in one paragraph · the objections answered (from the conversion path's Act stage) · the one action (button text: imperative and specific) · the lead magnet, if any · the form fields (as few as the action needs).
3. **Principles:** the Act stage's principles, each on its true basis (reciprocity only if the lead magnet exists; urgency only with a real date).
4. **Tracking:** the page must read the tag scheme's parameters; name the form or analytics setting as a client (or our) setup action.
5. **Image slots:** `landing-hero`, and one per proof point if the proof is visual. Describe each for `08 - visuals/`.

## Output template

The metadata block, then each section under its name, then `Setup actions: …`.

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* Headline: "Festival gifts your clients will keep." · Action: "Request the corporate catalogue" · Lead magnet: the festival catalogue PDF · Fields: name, company, work email, team size.

## Self-check

- [ ] Is there exactly one action?
- [ ] Is every proof point in hand?
- [ ] Are only as many form fields as the action needs?

## Traps

- **Two buttons.** "Catalogue" and "Call us" split the click.
- **Claims the posts didn't earn.** The page repeats what the pieces proved; it doesn't add new promises.
