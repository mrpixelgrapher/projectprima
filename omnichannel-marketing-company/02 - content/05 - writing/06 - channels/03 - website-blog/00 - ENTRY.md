# Website blog

**Purpose:** the piece on the client's own site, where search finds it and the destination is one click away. If the blog is the long-form home, it carries the full article; otherwise a short post that converts and points to the full piece.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `25-writing/<P>/06-article.md`, `07-seeds.md` (core insight); the piece brief (`24-planning/briefs/<P>.md`: keyword, links, CTA, voice); `22-strategy/04-channel-plan.md` (the long-form home and the destination); `01 - substack/article-template.md` (the long-form layout).
- **Writes:** `25-writing/<P>/channels/website-blog.md`.
- **Done when:** the variant chosen by the table is written, with its SEO block and one CTA.

## Procedure

1. **Choose the variant.**

   | The channel plan's long-form home | Write |
   |---|---|
   | The website blog | The **full article**, laid into `../01 - substack/article-template.md`, with every LINK and GRAPHIC marker resolved |
   | Anything else | A **summary post**: 300–600 words (the core insight, why it matters, what to do), the destination's CTA, and a link to the full piece |

2. **SEO block** at the top: title ≤ 70 characters with the keyword near the front; meta description 150–160 characters (the problem and the promise); URL slug; 3–5 keywords, the confirmed one first.
3. **One CTA only:** the brief's, with its tagged link to the destination. Imperative and specific ("Request the corporate catalogue").
4. **Image slot `website-hero`** (and the article's `featured` and `inline-N` slots for the full article). Describe each for `08 - visuals/` (they become image-factory prompts).
5. **Missing facts** (the form URL, a phone number) are `[CLIENT TO SUPPLY: …]`.

## Output template

The metadata block from `../00 - ENTRY.md` (add `Variant: full article | summary post`), the SEO block, then the copy.

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* Summary post for P2 `real-or-fake`: title "How HR teams check a gemstone gift in 60 seconds" (48 characters); CTA "See a real lab report in the catalogue →" linking to the landing page with `utm_source=website`.

## Self-check

- [ ] Was the variant chosen by the table?
- [ ] Is the SEO block within its limits?
- [ ] Is there exactly one CTA, matching the brief, with a tagged link?

## Traps

- **Two long-forms of one piece.** If the blog is not the long-form home, it summarises and converts; the long-form home develops the piece.
- **Invented contact details.**
