# 07 · Scripts

**Purpose:** for every piece the plan marks as video, write the script and a storyboard the video factory can refine into shots: what is said, what is shown, and in what order, beat by beat.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `24-planning/01-month-plan.md` (which pieces are video); the piece brief (video format, channel, date, language, voice); `25-writing/<P>/06-article.md`, `08-seeds.md`; `23-performance/03-virality.md` (hooks first); the topic's top-post sampling in `25-writing/arena-dossier-1/`.
- **Writes:** `25-writing/<P>/scripts/<V-id>.md` (one per video; V-id = `V<P number><letter>`, e.g. `V2a`).
- **Done when:** every video in the plan has a script and a storyboard whose frames cover every second of the script, or the plan has no video (write "no video this month" in `00-piece-index.md`).

## Procedure

1. **Format** from the brief: platform, length (e.g. 30–60 s reel), aspect ratio, language.
2. **Script, in beats:**

   | Beat | Time | Job |
   |---|---|---|
   | Hook | 0–3 s | Stop the scroll: a hook form from the virality design, said and shown at once |
   | Setup | 3–10 s | The problem in the buyer's words |
   | Turn | middle | The one thing from the spine, with one concrete particular |
   | Proof | before the end | One proof in hand, shown |
   | Call to action | last 3–5 s | The piece's CTA, spoken and on screen |

   Each beat: the voice-over (exact words, in the voiceprint), the on-screen text (exact), and the visual intent (what the viewer must see, in one line).
3. **Storyboard:** one numbered frame per visual change: duration, what the frame shows (subject, setting, action), the on-screen text, and how it moves to the next frame ("cut", "the camera pushes in", "stop-motion: the stones assemble"). The video factory turns each frame into shots and stills.
4. **Timing check:** the voice-over read aloud at a natural pace fits the length (about 2.5 words per second).
5. **Seed discipline:** one video, one seed. Never the whole article.

## Output template

    # Script <V-id> — <P>-<slug> · <platform>, <length>, <aspect>, <language>
    ## Beats
    | Beat | Time | Voice-over | On-screen text | Visual intent |
    ## Storyboard
    | Frame | Time | Shows | On-screen text | Move to next |
    Read-aloud time: … s

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* V2a · Instagram reel, 35 s, 9:16 · Hook (0–3 s): VO "Your festival gift might be fake." · on screen "Real or fake?" · visual: two identical blue stones side by side. Frame 1 (0–3 s): the two stones on white marble; move: the camera slides to the left stone.

## Self-check

- [ ] Does the hook land in the first 3 seconds?
- [ ] Does the read-aloud time fit the length?
- [ ] Does every storyboard frame say how it moves to the next?

## Traps

- **The article, read aloud.** A video carries one seed.
- **Frames that can't be pictured.** "Trust" can't be shot; "a lab report held up to the camera" can.
