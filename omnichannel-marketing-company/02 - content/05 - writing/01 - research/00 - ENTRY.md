# 01 · Research

**Purpose:** know each piece's topic out to its boundary before a word is drafted, then compress that knowledge into the one argument the piece will make. Research is done by ARENA; this stage asks precisely, maps what returns, and builds the spine.

Run the three sub-stages in order. Each folder's `00 - ENTRY.md` is its instruction.

| # | Sub-stage | One job | Writes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01 - request/` | One ARENA request for all the month's pieces: the boundary questions and the top-post sampling per topic (H3); hold | `25-writing/arena-request-1.md`, `25-writing/00-piece-index.md` |
| 2 | `02 - boundary/` | Per piece: the source ledger, the seven-sector boundary map and the observations, from the dossier | `25-writing/<P>/01-sources.md`, `02-boundary-map.md`, `03-observations.md` |
| 3 | `03 - spine/` | Per piece: the one thing no source says, the claims that carry it, the evidence behind each | `25-writing/<P>/04-spine.md` |

**Done when:** every piece in the month plan has a spine whose originality check says NONE, or the task is on HOLD for a second ARENA request or an operator decision (a pillar conflict).
