# 02 · Calendar

**Purpose:** put every post of the month on a date: each piece's variants on every channel, spread so that each channel keeps its cadence and no piece floods one day. This calendar is what packaging schedules and publishing follows.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `24-planning/01-month-plan.md`; `22-strategy/04-channel-plan.md` (cadence); `23-performance/03-virality.md` (days and times), `04-tests.md`, `05-tracking.md` (tag scheme).
- **Writes:** `24-planning/02-calendar.md`.
- **Done when:** every channel has exactly its monthly quantity of posts, each with a date, time, piece, seed, test variant and tagged link.

## Procedure

1. **Deploy each core piece over 4 weeks** from its start date: week 1, core insight (the long-form goes up, plus each channel's first variant); week 2, process; week 3, implications and discussion; week 4, framework and recap. Seeds come from the piece's brief.
2. **Stagger:** each piece starts at least 1 week after the one before it on the same topic, and no channel gets two posts from the same piece on the same day.
3. **Fill each channel** to its monthly quantity (engagement) at the cadence and times in the virality design. If a channel has more slots than the pieces' variants fill, the extra slots take standalone seeds (a framework, a save-worthy checklist) from the month's pieces.
4. **Mark test variants** on the posts that carry them, with an equal number of posts per variant.
5. **Tag every link** with the scheme from tracking (`utm_content=<P>-<variant>`).
6. **Video posts** (if included): the date each video posts, and which channel.

## Output template

    # Calendar — <client> · <month>
    | Date | Time | Channel | Piece | Seed | Variant | Format | Link (tagged) | Posted by |
    ## Per channel
    | Channel | Posts planned | Engagement quantity |

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* `| 2026-11-03 | 12:30 | Instagram | P1 | core insight | T1-A | carousel 7 | …&utm_content=P1-a | client |`

## Self-check

- [ ] Does each channel's count equal its engagement quantity?
- [ ] Is every post dated, timed and tagged?
- [ ] Are test variants balanced?

## Traps

- **All pieces in week 1.** The calendar is a month, not a launch day.
