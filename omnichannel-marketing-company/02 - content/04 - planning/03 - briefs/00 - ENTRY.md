# 03 · Briefs

**Purpose:** write one brief per piece containing everything writing needs to research, draft, voice and shape that piece without reopening the strategy or the performance design. Writers work from the brief.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `24-planning/01-month-plan.md`, `02-calendar.md`; `21-discovery/07-client-brief.md`, `06-voiceprint.md`; `22-strategy/*.md`; `23-performance/06-performance-design.md` (month task: the client-folder copies, per `00 - control/01 - law/TASK_CONTRACT.md` §6); `piece-brief-template.md` in this folder.
- **Writes:** `24-planning/briefs/<P>-<slug>.md` (one per piece) and `24-planning/03-brief-index.md`.
- **Done when:** every piece in the plan has a brief with every template section filled, and the index lists them all.

## Procedure

1. **Copy `piece-brief-template.md`** once per piece, into `24-planning/briefs/<P>-<slug>.md`.
2. **Fill every section by copying, not summarising,** where the template says *copy*: the position, the pillar, the buyer profile, the voiceprint, the words to use and never use, the limits, and the performance rules for writing. The drafter needs the exact text.
3. **Fill the piece-specific sections** from the plan and calendar: the question, stage, type, path job and CTA, format, keyword, links, channels with their dates, test variant, and video (if the piece becomes one).
4. **Image slots.** List the image slots each channel requires (`02 - content/05 - writing/08 - visuals/00 - ENTRY.md` has the slot table). Every still goes to the image factory.
5. **Write the index:** one row per brief: P, slug, type, question, first date, file.

## Output template

Use `piece-brief-template.md`. The index:

    # Brief index — <task id>
    | P | Slug | Type | Question | First date | File |

## Worked example

Gemstone task, brief `P2-real-or-fake` *(illustrative)*: the question is "How can a firm tell a real gemstone gift from a fake?"; the stage is considering; path job: trust; the pillar is "Every stone can be verified" (proof: a lab report with each piece [client]); the buyer profile (HR lead) and the voiceprint are copied in full; the CTA is "see a real lab report in the catalogue" with the tagged landing-page link; the links go to `P1-why-gifts-get-ignored`.

## Self-check

- [ ] Does every plan row have a brief, and does the index list them all?
- [ ] Could a drafter write the piece from the brief alone?
- [ ] Are the buyer profile, pillar and voiceprint copied verbatim?

## Traps

- **"See the strategy."** The writer works from the brief. Copy.
- **Briefs that prescribe the argument.** The spine is built in `02 - content/05 - writing/01 - research/` from research. The brief gives the question, not the answer.
