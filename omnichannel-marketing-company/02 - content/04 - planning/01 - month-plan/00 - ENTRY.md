# 01 · Month plan

**Purpose:** decide the month's pieces: the topics, the angles on each topic that together lead readers to the destination, and the standalone pieces. Each piece is the one question it answers for one buyer, with its job on the conversion path, so research knows what to find and writing knows what the piece is for.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `CONTEXT.md`; `clients/<client>/engagement.md` (quantities this month); `22-strategy/02-messages.md`, `03-destination.md`, `04-channel-plan.md`, `05-objectives.md` (month task: `clients/<client>/strategy.md`); `23-performance/01-results.md`, `06-performance-design.md`; `21-discovery/05-creator-library.md` (month task: `clients/<client>/creators.md`); last month's plan in `clients/<client>/done/` (month task).
- **Writes:** `24-planning/01-month-plan.md`.
- **Done when:** the number of core pieces equals the engagement's; every piece has an id, slug, type, topic, pillar, buyer, stage, question, path job, channels, keyword candidate and links; and the plan follows the rules below.

## Procedure

1. **The month.** Engagement route: the first month after acceptance. Month route: the month after the one being run. Write it at the top.
2. **Look back** (month route): list what last month published, the "more of / less of" from the results, and any topic still open.
3. **Topics.** Choose 1–2 topics for the month from the pillars, favouring the "more of" list and the creator library's "openings for us". Never a saturated angle.
4. **Pieces.** The engagement's number of core pieces, split into two types:

   | Type | What it is | Share of the month |
   |---|---|---|
   | Funnel angle | One of several pieces on the same topic, each from a different angle (a different buyer question or stage), all ending at the destination's action | At least half |
   | Standalone | A piece on its own question, for reach or authority, still with the destination's call to action | The rest |

   Across the month, cover the stages stop → engage → trust → act at least once each.
5. **Frame each piece as one question** for one buyer at one stage (e.g. *aware:* "why do festival gifts get ignored?"; *considering:* "how can you tell a real gemstone gift from a fake?"; *deciding:* "what does ordering 100 gifts involve?"). No outline; research finds the answer.
6. **Path job.** For each piece, its stage on the conversion path and its call to action, from the performance design.
7. **Channels.** Every piece goes to every channel in the plan (`00 - control/01 - law/OMNICHANNEL_ENFORCEMENT.md`). If the engagement includes video, name which pieces also become a video, up to the engagement's number.
8. **Keyword candidate:** one phrase the buyer would search, from their words in the brief.
9. **Internal links:** each piece links to earlier pieces that exist (this month's or earlier months'). The first piece of a new topic is excused; later pieces link to at least 1, from the third on to at least 2.
10. **Tests.** Assign each test in the performance design to the pieces that carry its variants.

## Output template

    # Month plan — <client> · <month>
    Look back: … (month route)
    Topics: …
    | P | Slug | Type | Topic | Pillar | Buyer | Stage | Question | Path job · CTA | Video? | Keyword candidate | Links to | Test variant |
    Channels for every piece: …

## Worked example

Gemstone task, first month *(illustrative)*, topic "choosing a festival gift that gets kept":

| P | Slug | Type | Stage | Question | Path job · CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | why-gifts-get-ignored | funnel angle | aware | Why do most festival gifts get ignored, and what makes one kept? | stop → "see what HR teams re-order" |
| P2 | real-or-fake | funnel angle | considering | How can a firm tell a real gemstone gift from a fake? | trust → "see a real lab report in the catalogue" |
| P3 | ordering-100-gifts | funnel angle | deciding | What does ordering 100 gifts before a festival involve? | act → "request the corporate catalogue" |

## Self-check

- [ ] Does the number of core pieces equal the engagement's?
- [ ] Are at least half the pieces funnel angles on the month's topic, and are all four path stages covered?
- [ ] Is every piece a single question with a path job and a CTA to the destination?
- [ ] Do the links follow the rule?

## Traps

- **Outlines disguised as a plan.** Headings belong to drafting, after research.
- **Pieces that go nowhere.** A piece with no call to the destination fails the job.
- **Ignoring last month.** A month plan that doesn't cite the results repeats what didn't work.
