# Research Plan — company-level research

Tier 2. Created: 2026-09-28, session S002.
Research law (handoff §2.4): external research starts with a brief, not browsing. Parallel lanes are legal only once a lane map exists (below). Every source is logged in `02-sourcing/source_ledger.md` as url · date · claim · confidence · implication. Contradictions are surfaced, never flattened.
Scope: **business** research that the "who" and "need" stations read. Article research is per piece and lives in `01 - writing-department/03 - research/research-[slug]/` (`00-control/ASSEMBLY_LINE.md` station S3).

## Brief

- **Question.** For a founder or small-business owner making a professional marketing decision (buyer type fixed by input I-002), what is the specific painful situation with fragmented channels? What have they already tried? How do they buy help? What would make an audit-first offer credible to them?
- **Why now.** `01-foundation/problem.md` and `01-foundation/value-proposition.md` hold only thesis-level claims (L-001) plus claims tagged PENDING on this research.
- **When it runs.** Each lane runs as part of the station that reads it, per the line law (I-007). L1 is station S1b's own research: it needs only the buyer type, which I-002 fixes, so it may run now. L2–L3 are station S2's research and run once S1 is FILLED. L4 feeds instructions, not claims, and may run any time.
- **Not in scope.** Pricing decisions (I-003: priced after the proof run). Research may collect price *norms* as context, but it never sets the number.
