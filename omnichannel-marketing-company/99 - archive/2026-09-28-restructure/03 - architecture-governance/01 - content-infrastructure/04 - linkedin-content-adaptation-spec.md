# 04 — LinkedIn Content Adaptation Spec

Type: platform spec

## Role in Ecosystem

Strategic amplification channel. Not a publishing destination — a professional authority layer that converts invisible expertise into a visible, searchable body of work.

## Content Types

| Type | Frequency | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Thought leadership post | 2/week | Insight → evidence → question. 200-400 words. |
| Case study breakdown | 1/week | Problem → methodology → result → lesson. 300-500 words. |
| Framework share | 1/week | Named framework + visual + application example. |
| Industry commentary | 1/week | Current event + expert perspective + implication. |
| Contrarian insight | Bi-weekly | Common assumption → why it's wrong → evidence → alternative. |

## Tone Guide

- Professional but not corporate-speak
- First person ("I've found that..." not "It has been observed that...")
- Evidence-driven (every claim backed by specific example or data)
- Question-ending (invite engagement, don't lecture)
- No hashtags spam (max 3-5 relevant hashtags)

## Adaptation Rules

1. **Never copy-paste** from Substack or other platforms. Rewrite entirely.
2. **Lead with the insight**, not the process. LinkedIn rewards outcomes over methodology.
3. **Use line breaks** every 2-3 sentences. LinkedIn's feed truncates long blocks.
4. **First 3 lines are the hook.** They appear before "See more." Make them count.
5. **Include 1-2 custom graphics** per post. Framework diagrams, before/after comparisons, or data visualizations.
6. **Link in comments, not post body.** LinkedIn's algorithm deprioritizes posts with external links.

## Vertical-Specific Adaptation

| Vertical | LinkedIn Positioning | Content Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Legal services | Subject-matter authority | Case analysis, regulatory commentary, dispute resolution frameworks |
| Consulting | Demonstrable expertise | Methodology breakdowns, client outcome frameworks (no client names) |
| Creative/art | Visual portfolio + process | Design thinking, brand strategy frameworks, creative process documentation |

## Growth Metrics

| Metric | Target | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Profile views | +20%/month | Consistent posting, keyword-rich headline and about section |
| Connection requests | +10/week | Thought leadership creates inbound interest |
| Newsletter subscribers | +5%/month | Every post funnels to Substack via comment link |
| Inbound inquiries | Track monthly | Authority posts generate consulting/speaking opportunities |
