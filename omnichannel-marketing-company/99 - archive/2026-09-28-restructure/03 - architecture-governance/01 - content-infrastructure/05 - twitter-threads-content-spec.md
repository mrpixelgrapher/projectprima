# 05 — Twitter/X and Threads Content Spec

Type: platform spec

## Twitter/X: Process and Thought Leadership

### Content Focus
How outcomes were achieved. Methodology, decision-making frameworks, research systems, execution mechanics. Pull back the curtain on the intellectual models behind the work.

### Thread Structure
```
Tweet 1 (Hook): Contrarian claim or surprising result. Under 280 chars.
Tweet 2-4: Process breakdown — step by step how it was done.
Tweet 5-7: Framework or mental model that made it possible.
Tweet 8-10: Lessons learned, what would change next time.
Tweet 11-12: Link to full article + call to subscribe.
```

### Standalone Tweets
- Methodology observations (1-2 sentences, dense insight)
- Framework names with one-line definitions
- Before/after comparisons from research-first production
- "What I learned" reflections from content creation process

### Frequency
- 1 thread per week (from Substack decomposition)
- 3-5 standalone tweets per week
- Engagement: reply to 5 relevant accounts daily

---

## Threads: Implications and Discussion

### Content Focus
Real-world consequences, practical outcomes, behavioral changes. Expand on what the ideas mean for the reader's work and life.

### Post Structure
```
Opening: The implication (what changes because of this insight)
Body: 2-3 concrete examples of the implication in practice
Close: Question that invites discussion
```

### Frequency
- 3-4 posts per week
- Each post: 100-300 words + 1 strong graphic
- Engage with 10 replies per post

### Key Difference from Twitter/X
Twitter/X = how it was done (process). Threads = what it means (implications). Same underlying research, different lens.
