# Twitter/X Thread Template

## Thread Topic: [Process breakdown / Methodology / Framework / Lesson learned]

### Tweet 1 (Hook — under 280 chars)

[Contrarian claim OR surprising result. This is what stops the scroll.]

🧵

### Tweet 2 (Context)

[Why this matters. The problem or question that led to the work.]

### Tweet 3-5 (Process)

[Step-by-step breakdown of HOW the result was achieved.]
[Each tweet = one step. Specific, not generic.]

### Tweet 6-8 (Framework)

[The mental model or framework that made this possible.]
[Name it. Define it in one sentence. Give an example.]

### Tweet 9-10 (Lessons)

[What was learned. What would change next time. What surprised you.]

### Tweet 11 (Call to Action)

[Link to full article on Substack.]
[One sentence: why reading the full piece is worth their time.]

### Tweet 12 (Engagement)

[Question for the audience. "Have you tried this?" or "What's your approach to X?"]

---

## Adaptation Checklist

- [ ] First tweet is the hook — contrarian or surprising (under 280 chars)
- [ ] Thread has clear progression (context → process → framework → lessons)
- [ ] Every tweet is self-contained (can be screenshot-shared independently)
- [ ] Process tweets are specific ("I did X" not "you should do X")
- [ ] Framework has a NAME (makes it shareable and referenceable)
- [ ] Final tweet links to Substack article
- [ ] 5-12 tweets total
- [ ] Tone: methodology exposure, pulling back the curtain
