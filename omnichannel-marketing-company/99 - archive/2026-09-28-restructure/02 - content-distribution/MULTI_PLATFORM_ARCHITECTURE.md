## Hub-and-spoke architecture (complete view)

```
Writing Department (research-first article)
    ↓
Substack Hub (canonical master source, IP repository)
    ↓
    ├── LinkedIn Layer (B2B thought-leadership derivative; executives / clients / recruiters / decision-makers)
    ├── Twitter/X Layer (process / methodology derivative; expertise-curious audiences)
    ├── Threads Layer (implications / discussion derivative; outcomes-curious audiences)
    ├── Facebook Layer (demographic-reach derivative; mature-reader / business-owner / investor audiences)
    └── Personal Website (executive summary + conversion layer routing back to Substack)
```

**Direction of flow:** every arrow is one-way. Substack is canonical; every downstream layer is a discovery mechanism that funnels audience back to Substack.

## Central content repository principle

The central content repository is Substack. It functions as the canonical source of truth:

- Every major research project, long-form article, case study, framework, tutorial, insight, or intellectual asset originates on Substack.
- Substack is the platform where audience ownership is maintained and subscriptions can be captured directly.
- Long-form Substack content is optimized for depth, search visibility, evergreen relevance, and long-term discoverability.
- All downstream content assets are derived from Substack; none compete with it.

The personal website functions as an executive summary layer that organizes knowledge, highlights expertise, and routes visitors toward Substack. It does not compete with the newsletter ecosystem — it is a navigational and conversion mechanism that increases discoverability while consolidating audience attention.

## Translation, not syndication

This is the single discipline that unifies all four layers (per `.\WORKING_RULES.md` Rule 1):

> Every derivative asset must be rewritten according to the communication norms, audience expectations, and content consumption patterns of the destination platform. Identical cross-posting is explicitly prohibited because each platform rewards different forms of expression and attracts different audience psychologies. The goal is not syndication but translation. Each platform should communicate the same underlying idea through a different lens.

**Violation:** cross-posting identical content across any two platforms is a governance violation.

## Substack-is-the-anchor principle

This is the single funnel discipline that unifies all four layers (per `.\WORKING_RULES.md` Rule 2):

> All distribution flows FROM Substack TO platforms, never the reverse. The Substack article is the canonical source. Platform derivatives are discoveries that funnel audience BACK to Substack.

Every layer declares canonical-link-to-Substack in its metadata. Every layer's derivative ends with a funnel to Substack (link placement varies by platform discipline).

## Seed-to-platform mapping (complete table)

Every Substack article decomposes into 6 seeds. Each seed flows to the platform best suited to develop it:

| Seed | Primary platform | Secondary platform |
|---|---|---|
| Core insight | LinkedIn (thought leadership) | Medium (condensed takeaway) |
| Process breakdown | Twitter/X (how outcomes were achieved) | — |
| Contrarian perspective | LinkedIn + Twitter/X | — |
| Framework | LinkedIn (educational) | Instagram (visual carousel — not yet built) |
| Implication / discussion | Threads (deeper conversation) | Facebook (demographic-reach derivative) |
| Visual concept | Facebook (visual storytelling) | Instagram (visual framework — not yet built) |

**Seed-split discipline:** when a seed flows to two platforms, each platform develops a different angle from the same seed. Cross-posting the same angle is prohibited.

## Audience coverage (no overlaps, no gaps)

| Platform | Primary audience | What it captures the others miss |
|---|---|---|
| Substack | Core audience (direct subscribers) | Audience ownership, full IP archive |
| LinkedIn | B2B professionals, active career stage | Executives, clients, recruiters, decision-makers |
| Twitter/X | Process / methodology audiences | Expertise-curious readers who want the "how" |
| Threads | Outcomes / discussion audiences | Discussion-oriented readers who want implications |
| Facebook | Mature readers, approaching-retirement professionals, business owners, investors, high-income segments | Demographics not on LinkedIn / Twitter / Threads |

**Coverage rule:** the four derivative layers together must reach every audience segment the brand serves. If a segment is missing, a new layer must be added to this architecture before that segment is targeted.

## 30-day deployment framework (cross-platform)

Every Substack article generates content across 4 weeks, not a single publication spike:

| Week | Focus | Platform |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Core insight exposure | LinkedIn (main derivative) + Substack (canonical) |
| 2 | Process / demographic reach | Twitter/X (process thread) + Facebook (demographic derivative) |
| 3 | Discussion / contrarian angle | Threads (implications post) + LinkedIn (secondary contrarian) |
| 4 | Recap + Substack funnel | All platforms (recap posts with explicit Substack link in body) |

**No-same-day discipline:** no two derivative platforms publish for the same article on the same calendar day. Space them across the 30-day framework.

## Unified visual identity + custom graphics pipeline

Every major idea, framework, model, methodology, and insight is transformed into reusable visual assets that can be adapted across platforms while maintaining brand consistency. Visual assets are produced by the Media Department (per `.\..\design-visual-artist-brand\01 - foundation\13-media-department\02 - media-department\OUTPUT_CONTRACT.md`).

**Visual-as-intellectual-compression principle:** graphics are not decoration. They act as intellectual compression mechanisms that allow complex concepts to be understood rapidly and shared organically.

| Platform | Visual expectation |
|---|---|
| Substack | Featured image + inline visual assets per article |
| LinkedIn | Custom graphics + visual frameworks + branded content assets |
| Twitter/X | Hero image for thread; optional process infographic |
| Threads | Mandatory custom graphic illustrating the implication |
| Facebook | Compelling custom graphic (storytelling-oriented, high production value) |

**Brand-consistency rule:** every visual asset, regardless of platform, must be recognizable as the same brand. Platform adaptation does not mean brand deviation.

## Self-reinforcing distribution engine

The strategic loop the architecture creates:

```
High-quality research (Writing Dept)
    → Long-form authority (Substack)
        → Platform-specific content (4 layers)
            → Audience attention
                → Redirected into owned channels (Substack + website)
                    → Strengthened visibility and credibility of previous work
                        → Compounding SEO value
                            → More audience attention
                                → (loop continues)
```

**Feedback discipline:** every Substack article names its upstream research input and its downstream derivative outputs. Every derivative names its Substack source and its platform funnel. The loop is observable end-to-end.

## Multi-vertical deployment (reusable business asset)

This architecture is not a single-brand marketing strategy. The same distribution engine deploys across multiple professional domains:

| Vertical | Substack focus | LinkedIn focus | Twitter/X focus | Threads focus | Facebook focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Legal services | Legal frameworks, case analysis | Subject-matter authority for client acquisition | Process / methodology of legal reasoning | Implications of legal shifts | High-value client demographic reach |
| Consulting / paralegal advisory | Demonstrable expertise archive | Decision-maker visibility | Process of advisory engagements | Outcomes for clients | Business-owner demographic |
| Creative agency / media brand | Visual storytelling archive | Portfolio visibility | Process of creative production | Implications of creative choices | Mature-audience portfolio reach |
| Research | Deep-research archive | Research-to-decision translation | Research methodology | Implications of findings | Investor / high-income audience |
| Branding / design | Brand-thinking archive | Brand authority | Process of brand building | Implications of brand choices | Business-owner audience |

**Deployment rule:** each vertical maintains its own Substack repository, its own audience pathways, and its own platform-specific communication style. The underlying distribution engine (this architecture + the 4 layer subfolders) is shared.

## AI-era strategic window

The source declares: "the present period represents a temporary strategic window in which AI-assisted content can still be transformed into work that is perceived as genuinely human when supported by rigorous research. As AI detection systems continue to improve over the coming five to six years, the advantage will not belong to creators who generate the most content, but to those who use AI to accelerate a robust research-first methodology and build large archives of genuinely valuable work before the landscape changes."

**Distribution is a force multiplier for research, not a replacement for it.** This is the same principle named in `.\..\03 - architecture-governance\01 - content-infrastructure\RESEARCH_METHODOLOGY_REFERENCE.md` (the "AI as force multiplier" section). The research-first discipline is the non-negotiable that makes the distribution engine valuable regardless of future AI-detection shifts.
