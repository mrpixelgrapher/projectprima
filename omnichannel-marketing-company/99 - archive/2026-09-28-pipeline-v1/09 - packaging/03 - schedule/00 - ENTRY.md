# 03 · Schedule

**Purpose:** put every piece's 30-day deployment on one calendar the client can follow, day by day. Each week of a piece shows a different side of the same research.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `09-packaging/02-kit-index.md`; the parent's `04-planning/01-content-plan.md` (start weeks); `03-strategy/03-channel-plan.md` (the first spoke, cadence, who publishes).
- **Writes:** `09-packaging/03-schedule.md`.
- **Done when:** every kit file has a date (or a day number), and the rules below hold.

## Procedure

1. **Day 1** is the client's start date if they gave one [client]. Otherwise write "Day 1 = the day you publish the first piece", and use day numbers.
2. **Each piece's 30 days:**

   | Week | Focus | Channels |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | The core insight | The hub, then the first spoke on a later day that week |
   | 2 | Process | The process-seed channels (e.g. Twitter/X) and the demographic channels (e.g. Facebook) |
   | 3 | Implications and discussion | The implication channels (Threads, email), plus a second post on the first spoke if its cadence allows |
   | 4 | Framework and recap | The framework channels (Instagram, collateral use); the recap post with the hub link in the body |

   Use only the channels in the plan. Drop the weeks with no channel.
3. **Pieces start on their planned weeks,** staggered by at least a week.
4. **No same-day rule:** no two channels publish the same piece on the same day.
5. **Who publishes:** each row names who publishes, from the plan (the EXECUTION slot).

## Output template

    # Schedule — <parent id>
    Day 1 = …
    | Day / date | Piece | Channel | What (kit file) | Link placement | Who publishes |

## Self-check

- [ ] Does every kit file appear exactly once?
- [ ] Is no piece published on two channels on the same day?
- [ ] Is the hub always first for its piece?

## Traps

- **Everything on day 1.** That is a spike, not a deployment.
- **Scheduling channels the client can't run.** They aren't in the plan.
