# Website

**Purpose:** the conversion layer. On the client's website, this turns a visitor who is interested in the piece into the one action the objectives need.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `08-shaping/01-seeds.md` (core insight); `08-shaping/02-hub-metadata.md`; `00-brief.md` (CTA, voice); `03-strategy/03-channel-plan.md` (the hub choice, via CONTEXT).
- **Writes:** `08-shaping/03-channels/website.md`.
- **Done when:** the right variant is written, with one CTA.

## Procedure

1. **Choose the variant.**

   | Hub | Write |
   |---|---|
   | The client's own site blog | A **landing block** for the site: headline, sub-headline, 3 benefit bullets from the pillar, CTA button text, and one line linking to the article |
   | Anything else (Substack, newsletter) | A **summary page**: 300–600 words (the core insight, why it matters, what to do), one CTA, and a link to the full piece on the hub |

2. **One CTA only:** the brief's. Its wording is imperative and specific ("Request the corporate catalogue").
3. **Image slot `website-hero`.** Describe it for `04 - visuals/`.
4. **Missing facts** (the contact form URL, phone number) are `[CLIENT TO SUPPLY: …]`.

## Output template

The metadata block (add `Variant: landing block | summary page`), then the copy, section by section.

## Self-check

- [ ] Was the variant chosen by the table?
- [ ] Is there exactly one CTA, matching the brief?
- [ ] Are there no invented contact details?

## Traps

- **A second article.** The website summarises and converts; the hub develops the piece.
