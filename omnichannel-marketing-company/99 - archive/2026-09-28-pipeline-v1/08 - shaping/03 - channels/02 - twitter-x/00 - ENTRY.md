# Twitter/X thread

**Purpose:** a native thread that walks people who are curious about method through *how* the result is achieved, and ends with the hub link.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `08-shaping/01-seeds.md` (process, plus contrarian for the hook); `00-brief.md` (voice); `template.md` in this folder.
- **Writes:** `08-shaping/03-channels/twitter-x.md`.
- **Done when:** the thread has 5–12 numbered posts and meets every rule below.

## Procedure

1. **Post 1 is the hook:** under 280 characters; a contrarian claim or a surprising result, drawn from the contrarian seed.
2. **Posts 2–4:** why it matters, then the method, one step per post, specific ("we check X", not "you should check").
3. **Posts 5–8:** the framework or mechanism. Name it, define it in one line, and give one example.
4. **Posts 9–11:** lessons, edge cases, and what would change next time.
5. **The final post:** the hub link, plus one sentence on why the full piece is worth reading. **It is the only post with a link.**
6. **Self-contained posts:** each post should make sense if screenshotted alone.
7. **Image slot `twitter-hero`:** for post 1 or 2. Describe it for `04 - visuals/`.

## Output template

Use `template.md`: posts numbered 1/ … N/, under the metadata block.

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* 1/ "A lab report doesn't make a gemstone gift trustworthy. Being able to check it in 60 seconds does. Here's the check 🧵"

## Self-check

- [ ] Are there 5–12 posts, with post 1 under 280 characters?
- [ ] Does only the final post carry a link?
- [ ] Is the framework named?
- [ ] Is every post self-contained?
- [ ] Is it process-focused, not an outcomes pep talk (that's the Threads angle)?

## Traps

- **An article chopped into posts.** A thread is born a thread.
- **Links mid-thread.** They break the native feel, and are forbidden.
