# 05 - research

Status: BUILT (session S003, 2026-09-28)

**Purpose:** for one piece, research its question out to the full boundary of the topic, then compress that into a spine: the one thing the piece knows that no single source says, and the claims and evidence that carry it. Drafting writes from the spine and nothing else.

**Use when:** a child task arrives from `04 - planning` (it carries `00-brief.md`), or a piece is sent back from `07 - review`. **Child tasks only.**

## Rules this node lives by

1. **Research before drafting.** No article prose is written here: not a lead, not an outline.
2. **Research is permanent.** A finished task keeps its research folder. Before searching, reuse earlier research on the same topic from `10 - delivery/done/`.
3. **Mark it, don't invent it.** A particular you can't source is marked `[VERIFY]`, and may not be used as evidence in the spine.
