# 01 · Content plan

**Purpose:** decide exactly which pieces the package contains, and in what order. Each piece is framed as the one question it answers for one buyer, so research knows what to research.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `CONTEXT.md` (the 01–03 sections); `03-strategy/02-messages.md`, `03-channel-plan.md`, `04-objectives.md`; the approved changes in `client/approval-strategy.md`.
- **Writes:** `04-planning/01-content-plan.md`.
- **Done when:** every piece has a slug, pillar, buyer, stage, question, hub format, channels, keyword candidate, cluster role and start week, and the plan satisfies the rules below.

## Procedure

1. **Count the pieces.**

   | Package (from the frame) | Pieces |
   |---|---|
   | Launch set | 1 **cornerstone** (argues the position in full) + 1 **supporting** piece for each pillar the cornerstone can't carry in depth. Total ≤ 5 |
   | Name + positioning, then launch set | The same. The cornerstone also introduces the new name |
   | Recurring batch | Pieces per month × months, as approved. Rotate through the pillars |

2. **Frame each piece as a question.** Write the one question it answers for its buyer, at their stage. Examples of the pattern: *aware:* "why do festival gifts get ignored?"; *considering:* "how can you tell a real gemstone gift from a fake?"; *deciding:* "what does ordering 100 gifts actually involve?". A piece is a question for research, never an outline. No body text is written at this node.
3. **Hub format.** Write "long-form article, 2,000–5,000 words" for the cornerstone. Write "article, 1,200–2,500 words" for supporting pieces, unless the channel plan says otherwise.
4. **Channels.** List every channel in the channel plan. Every piece goes to every channel in the plan (`00 - control/01 - law/OMNICHANNEL_ENFORCEMENT.md`).
5. **Keyword candidate.** One phrase the buyer would search, taken from their words in the brief. Research verifies it.
6. **Cluster.** All pieces of one package form one topic cluster. The cornerstone is the cluster index and ships first.
   - **Internal links:** a piece links to earlier pieces that exist. The first is excused, the second links to the first, and from the third on each links to at least 2.
7. **Order and timing.**
   - The cornerstone starts week 1.
   - Each later piece starts at least 1 week after the one before it, so no two pieces are in their week 1 at the same time.
   - Each piece runs its own 30-day deployment: week 1 core insight (hub + first spoke), week 2 process, week 3 implications and discussion, week 4 framework and recap. Packaging builds the calendar.

## Output template

    # Content plan — <task id>
    Package: … · Pieces: N · Cluster: <name>
    | # | Slug | Role | Pillar | Buyer | Stage | Question | Hub format | Keyword candidate | Links to | Start week |
    Channels for every piece: <list from the channel plan>

## Self-check

- [ ] Does the piece count follow the table?
- [ ] Is every piece a single question for one buyer at one stage?
- [ ] Do the internal links follow the first-piece rule?
- [ ] Are the start weeks staggered by at least 1 week?

## Traps

- **Outlines disguised as a plan.** Headings and bullet content belong to drafting, after research.
- **A calendar instead of a plan.** "3 posts a week" is not a piece.
- **Too many pieces.** More than 5 in a launch set thins every one of them.
