# 01 — Research-First Content Production

Type: CE governance rule

## Rule

No content drafting begins until a Knowledge Sufficiency threshold is met. Research is the primary value-generating activity; drafting is the final synthesis step, not the discovery mechanism.

## CE Implementation

This rule is already partially encoded in CE's Gate 8 (Knowledge Sufficiency Evaluation). The governance extends it from task-level gating to content-production methodology:

### Two-Phase Workflow

**Phase 1 — Expansion (Research)**
- Map the full boundary of the topic: sources, perspectives, frameworks, contradictions, historical context, adjacent concepts
- Accumulate source material as reusable intellectual capital (not transient intermediates)
- Store research artifacts hierarchically: raw sources → extracted observations → synthesis
- Continue until contextual density supports original synthesis (not just "enough to write")

**Phase 2 — Compression (Drafting)**
- Synthesize accumulated research inward toward the core narrative
- Draft emerges from the research, not parallel to it
- Article becomes the visible top layer of a much larger knowledge structure

### Verification Checks

| Gate | Test | Fail Action |
|---|---|---|
| Research saturation | Can the topic's full boundary be named? | Return to Phase 1 |
| Source depth | Are there 3+ independent source categories? | Expand source acquisition |
| Original synthesis | Does the draft contain insights not present in any single source? | Deepen analysis |
| Traceability | Can every claim be traced to accumulated research? | Add sourcing |

### Anti-Patterns (detect and reject)

- **Top-down drafting:** Starting with an outline and filling in — research was skipped
- **Search-and-write:** Web search immediately followed by drafting — no accumulation phase
- **Collapsed phases:** Research and drafting happening simultaneously — the middle layer is missing
- **Single-source synthesis:** Drafting from one source — no original synthesis possible

### CE Integration Points

- **Gate 8 (KSE):** Already evaluates knowledge sufficiency before task execution. This rule extends KSE scoring to content-production tasks specifically.
- **Cognitive cognitive sequence:** When dispatching content-production tasks, the cognitive sequence should verify Phase 1 completion before generating drafting instructions.
- **Arena research:** When KSE scores LOW/CRITICAL for a content topic, Arena research IS the expansion phase. The Arena output becomes the research artifact.

### Reusable Research Assets

Every research effort should produce artifacts that survive beyond the article:
- Source collections (raw material for future outputs)
- Extracted observations and frameworks (reusable across articles)
- Synthesis notes (reasoning pathways, not just conclusions)

A future article, newsletter, social post, or client document should draw from the same research repository without requiring re-acquisition.
