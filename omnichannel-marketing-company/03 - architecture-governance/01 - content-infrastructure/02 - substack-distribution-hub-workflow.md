# 02 — Substack Distribution Hub Workflow

Type: workflow template

## Architecture

Hub-and-spoke model. Substack is the owned anchor; all other platforms are discovery spokes.

```
Research → Long-form article (Substack) → Platform derivatives → Audience capture → Substack
```

## Substack Newsletter Structure

### Per-Article Template

```markdown
# [Title — SEO-optimized, specific, benefit-driven]

## Lead (2-3 sentences)
Hook: the specific problem or question this article resolves.

## Body (2000-5000 words)
Research-first content per governance rule 01.
Subheadings every 300-400 words for scannability.
Internal links to related articles in the archive.

## Key Takeaways (3-5 bullets)
Compressed insights for sharing and reference.

## Call to Action
Subscribe link + one specific next article recommendation.
```

### Content Cluster Organization

Group articles into thematic clusters that strengthen each other's authority:
- Each cluster has a pillar article (comprehensive, evergreen)
- Supporting articles link to the pillar and to each other
- New articles always connect to at least one existing cluster

### Growth Mechanics

| Mechanic | Implementation |
|---|---|
| SEO | Every article targets 1 primary keyword + 3-5 secondary. Title, H1, first 100 words, meta description. |
| Internal linking | Minimum 3 links to other articles per piece. Pillar articles link to all supporting content. |
| Subscriber retention | Weekly cadence minimum. Each email previews next week's topic. |
| Archive value | Every article increases the searchable knowledge base. Older articles gain traffic as cluster authority grows. |

## Decomposition Workflow (post-publication)

After each Substack publication, produce platform derivatives within 48 hours:

| Platform | Derivation Type | Tone | Link Back |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Professional reframing | Executive, data-driven | Newsletter link in comments |
| Twitter/X | Process breakdown | Methodology, how-it-was-done | Thread ending with newsletter link |
| Threads | Implications discussion | Conversational, outcome-focused | Direct newsletter link |
| Medium | Condensed takeaway | Executive summary angle | Canonical link to Substack |
| Instagram | Visual framework | Carousel or single graphic | Bio link to newsletter |
| Facebook | Educational angle | Accessible, mature-audience tone | Direct newsletter link |

Each derivative is a NEW piece of content in that platform's native register — not a repost.
