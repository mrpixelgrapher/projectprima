# 06 — Facebook Demographic Content Spec

Type: platform spec

## Strategic Role

Audience acquisition layer for mature demographics (45+) with higher purchasing power. These readers are under-served on Twitter/X and Threads. They discover content through Facebook shares, groups, and the feed algorithm.

## Audience Personas

| Persona | Content Angle | Emotional Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Business owner (50+) | Operational frameworks, organizational design | "I wish I'd known this earlier" |
| Professional approaching retirement | Legacy building, knowledge transfer | "My experience has value" |
| Investor / high-net-worth | Strategic thinking, market analysis | "I see what others miss" |
| Established creative | Process refinement, portfolio methodology | "Refining my life's work" |

## Content Rules

1. **Accessible language.** No jargon without explanation. This audience values clarity over cleverness.
2. **Educational framing.** Position every post as teaching something valuable.
3. **Strong opening image.** Facebook's algorithm rewards visual engagement. Use custom graphics, not stock photos.
4. **Longer text OK.** Facebook allows and rewards longer posts (300-800 words) compared to Twitter/X.
5. **Direct link in post.** Unlike LinkedIn, Facebook doesn't penalize external links as heavily.
6. **Respect the audience.** This demographic has decades of experience. Don't explain basics they already know.

## Content Types

| Type | Frequency | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Framework explanation | 2/week | Named concept → why it matters → how to apply it |
| Experience-based insight | 1/week | Personal observation → broader principle → practical application |
| Educational carousel | 1/week | 5-8 slide image post breaking down a concept |
| Discussion prompt | 1/week | Open-ended question with expert context |

## Integration

Facebook posts are derivatives of Substack articles, same as all other platforms. But the adaptation must account for:
- Slower content consumption pace (more text, less rapid-fire)
- Higher value placed on practical, applicable knowledge
- Group sharing potential (content that's useful enough to share in professional groups)
- Comment engagement (this demographic engages in longer comment threads)
