# Questions for you — round 1

You asked us to: "do performance marketing for me for xyz". If we've misread that, tell us first.

## CQ1. What is xyz: a client of yours, your own performance-marketing business, or another business of yours? And what is its name?

Why we ask: it decides whose voice the content speaks in, and who it has to win over.
Options: a client of mine / my own marketing business / another business of mine / not sure
Needed by: planning the work and its price

## CQ2. By performance marketing, do you mean running paid ads, or content built to bring in measurable leads and sales without ad spend, or both?

Why we ask: we make organic content engineered to convert, and we measure it on results every month. We don't run paid ads, so if you need ads managed, that part stays with you or your team.
Options: paid ads / organic content built to convert / both / not sure
Needed by: now, before anything else

## CQ3. Is xyz already selling, or about to launch? What does it sell, in one line?

Why we ask: launching and growing need different plans, and the content must be about something a buyer can actually get.
Example answer: "Selling for 2 years: a meal-kit subscription for busy families in Pune"
Needed by: planning the work and its price

## CQ4. Should we post the content on xyz's accounts, or hand it to you ready to post?

Why we ask: posting for you is extra work we plan and price separately.
Options: you post it / we post it / not sure
Needed by: planning the work and its price

## What we'll assume unless you tell us otherwise

Nothing yet: each point above changes the work, so we'd rather ask.
