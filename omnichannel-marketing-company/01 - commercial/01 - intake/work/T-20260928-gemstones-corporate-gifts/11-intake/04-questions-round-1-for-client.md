# Questions for you — round 1

You told us: "I'm into precious gemstones selling and want to like build corporate gifts as a sub branch". If we've misread that, tell us first.

## CQ1. What would you like from us?

Why we ask: we're a marketing company. We can bring corporate buyers to your gift line with content. We don't design or source the gifts themselves.
Options: content that brings in corporate buyers / help setting up the gift line itself (products, sourcing, pricing) / both / not sure
Needed by: now, before anything else

## CQ2. Will the corporate gifts be sold under your current business name, or under a new name?

Why we ask: a new name means we first help you position it; your current name means we build on the reputation you already have.
Options: our current name / a new name / not sure
Needed by: planning the work and its price

## CQ3. What would the gifts be, and do they exist yet?

Why we ask: what we write depends on what a company can actually order, and at what price.
Example answer: "3 desk pieces with a gemstone set in silver, designs ready, not made yet, about 5,000–15,000 per piece"
Needed by: planning the work and its price

## CQ4. What would you like this to achieve in the first 3 months, and how will you know it worked?

Why we ask: we size the work to your goal and measure against it every month.
Example answer: "10 company orders before Diwali"
Needed by: planning the work and its price
