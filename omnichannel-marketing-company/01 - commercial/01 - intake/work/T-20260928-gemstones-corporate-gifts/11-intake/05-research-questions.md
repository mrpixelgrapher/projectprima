# Research questions — T-20260928-gemstones-corporate-gifts

These go to ARENA at `02 - content/01 - discovery/01 - requests/`; nothing is researched at intake.

## RQ1. How do companies in the target segment choose and buy corporate gifts?

Capture: who chooses, who approves and who pays; the occasions (festivals, client appreciation, employee milestones); lead times; typical budget per gift and order size — each with its source, sample and year · Sources: surveys of HR and procurement teams, corporate-gifting vendor reports, HR practitioner write-ups, forums · Needed by: 02 - content/01 - discovery · Condition: after CQ8 (which companies)

## RQ2. Who sells gemstone or fine-jewellery corporate gifts in the client's market, and how do they present themselves?

Capture: 5+ providers, their positioning in their own words (verbatim, with URLs), price ranges where stated, what buyers praise or complain about · Sources: company sites, marketplaces, review sites, LinkedIn pages · Needed by: 02 - content/02 - strategy · Condition: after CQ3 (the gifts) and CQ6 (where they sell)

## RQ3. What rules apply to selling gemstone gifts to companies in the client's market?

Capture: gemstone certification or disclosure rules, hallmarking for precious-metal settings, any company policy or tax limits on gift value · Sources: official and regulator sites first, then trade bodies · Needed by: 02 - content/02 - strategy · Condition: after CQ6 (where they sell)

## RQ4. What proof do corporate buyers expect before ordering premium gifts?

Capture: the evidence that most increases trust (certificates, samples, references, return terms), with how each was measured · Sources: buyer surveys, procurement guides, reviews and forums · Needed by: 02 - content/02 - strategy · Condition: after CQ8 (which companies)
