# 02 · Document

**Purpose:** write the proposal the client reads: what they said they want, exactly what they get each month, what it costs down to the work, what they must give us, and how to say yes.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `14-proposal/01-package.md`; `13-pricing/03-lines.md`; `CONTEXT.md` (`## 11-intake`); `01 - commercial/billing-profile.md`.
- **Writes:** `14-proposal/02-proposal-for-client.md` (a revision after changes: `02-proposal-v2-for-client.md`, and so on).
- **Done when:** every section of the template is filled in the client's language, and every number matches `01-package.md`.

## Procedure

1. **Open with their goal,** in their own words (from the slot board), and one line on how the work reaches it: research-backed organic content, built to spread and convert, driving traffic to one destination.
2. **Say what is not included:** paid ads. If they asked for "performance marketing", say that the performance is designed into organic content.
3. **List the deliverables** as the package fixes them: setup once; each month; add-ons they can switch on. Plain units ("12 LinkedIn posts a month").
4. **Show the price**: block totals in the main text; the itemised lines (deliverable → sub-tasks → hours × rate, plus costs) in an appendix, so they can see what the money buys.
5. **Say what we need from them:** approvals at strategy and at each month's plan; answers when asked; results each month (retainer); anything from ASSETS.
6. **Payment and acceptance:** the schedule; validity (14 days from sending); how to accept ("reply 'accepted', and name any add-ons you want").
7. **Write in the client's language** (LANGUAGE), plainly, with no marketing jargon they didn't use.

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* "What you want: 'want 10 company orders before diwali'. What we'll do: research what makes HR teams choose a gift, write six pieces a month that answer it, and send every reader to one landing page where they can order."

## Self-check

- [ ] Does every number equal the package's?
- [ ] Is paid advertising stated as not included?
- [ ] Can the client accept with one reply?

## Traps

- **Selling the process.** The client buys outcomes and deliverables; keep the method to one line.
- **A price with no appendix.** The company's promise is itemised work.
