# 02 · Breakdown

**Purpose:** break every deliverable into sub-tasks, each with exactly one kind of work and an effort in hours, so pricing can multiply each by a rate and invoices can list the work.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `12-scope/01-deliverables.md`; `effort-table.md` in this folder.
- **Writes:** `12-scope/02-breakdown.md`.
- **Done when:** every deliverable (setup, monthly, add-on) has its sub-tasks; every sub-task has a kind and hours from the effort table (or a stated reason for a different number); totals per deliverable and per kind are summed.

## Procedure

1. **For each deliverable,** copy its sub-task rows from `effort-table.md`, and multiply the hours per unit by the quantity.
2. **Adjust only by rule.** An effort different from the table needs a reason visible in the task: LANGUAGE lists two languages for that channel → × 1.5 for writing sub-tasks; F1 = NEW → strategy sub-tasks × 1.5 (naming). Any other change is noted for the operator's sign-off, not made silently.
3. **Number the sub-tasks** ST1, ST2 … in deliverable order.
4. **Sum** the hours per deliverable, per kind of work, and separately for setup, monthly base and each add-on.

## Output template

    # Breakdown — <task id>
    ## Sub-tasks
    | ST | Deliverable | Sub-task | Kind of work | Hours per unit | Units | Hours | Adjustment (rule) |
    ## Totals
    | Block | research | strategy | writing | visual prompting | video | publishing | account management | Total hours |
    | Setup | … |
    | Monthly base | … |
    | Add-on <D> | … |

## Worked example

Gemstone task, D1 core long-form piece × 6 *(illustrative)*:

| ST | Deliverable | Sub-task | Kind | Hours per unit | Units | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ST7 | D1 | Topic research request and dossier reading | research | 2 | 6 | 12 |
| ST8 | D1 | Draft, re-voice and channel shaping | writing | 4 | 6 | 24 |
| ST9 | D1 | Still prompts (3 per piece) | visual prompting | 0.75 | 6 | 4.5 |
| ST10 | D1 | Review and human check | writing | 1 | 6 | 6 |

## Self-check

- [ ] Does every deliverable have sub-tasks, and every sub-task exactly one kind of work?
- [ ] Is every hours figure from the effort table, or adjusted by a stated rule?
- [ ] Do the totals add up?

## Traps

- **Hours invented per task.** Guessing effort instead of using the table makes prices inconsistent between clients. Change the table (through lessons), not the task.
- **A sub-task with two kinds.** "Research and writing" can't be priced at one rate. Split it.
