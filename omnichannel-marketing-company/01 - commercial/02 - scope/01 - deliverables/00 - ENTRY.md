# 01 · Deliverables

**Purpose:** list everything the client would receive, as countable items, so pricing and the proposal work from the same fixed list. The next stage breaks each item into work.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `CONTEXT.md` (`## 11-intake`); `11-intake/02-frame.md`, `11-intake/06-slot-board.md`; `clients/<client>/profile.md`.
- **Writes:** `12-scope/01-deliverables.md`.
- **Done when:** every IN family from F3 has deliverables; every ASK family is an add-on candidate; every deliverable has a quantity, a unit and a "why" that cites the goal or the frame.

## Procedure

1. **Setup deliverables** (once, at the start of an engagement). Always: D-S1 client brief and creator library (discovery), D-S2 strategy with the destination (strategy), D-S3 performance design (performance). Add D-S4 **destination build** (the newsletter, landing page or site page where traffic lands) if CHANNELS says it doesn't exist yet. Add D-S5 **name and positioning** if F1 = NEW.
2. **Monthly deliverables**, one row per IN family and channel, from the cadence table:

   | Deliverable | Default quantity per month | Change it when |
   |---|---|---|
   | Core long-form piece (research-backed) | 4 (one a week) | GOAL has a date within 6 weeks → 6; budget below the base (pricing will tell) → 2 |
   | Destination piece (newsletter issue or landing-page update) | 4 | the destination is a landing page → 1 update a month |
   | Text-social variants (per channel IN) | 12 per channel (3 a week) | the client posts today at a lower rate on that channel → match it, minimum 8 |
   | Visual-social posts or carousels (per channel IN) | 8 per channel | same |
   | Stills (images, slides, covers) | 3 per core piece + 1 per visual post | — |
   | Video (script + storyboard → video factory) | 0 unless the video family is IN; then 4 | GOAL names reach or awareness → 8 |
   | Publishing (posting on the client's accounts) | only if F4 = CONTENT+PUBLISHING: every post above | — |
   | Monthly review and optimisation | 1 (retainer only) | — |

   The defaults are the company's starting cadence (S003). A deviation needs a "Change it when" row that matches, or the operator's instruction at sign-off.
3. **Add-on candidates.** One row per ASK family and per channel not IN: the same units, marked "add-on".
4. **Excluded, and said so:** paid ads (the company does organic content only); anything the request asked for that no family covers, named.
5. **Engagement shape.** From F2: LAUNCH → a one-off (the setup plus one month's deliverables), and a retainer option; ONGOING → a retainer. The client chooses at the proposal.

## Self-check

- [ ] Does every quantity come from the cadence table or a matching change rule?
- [ ] Are paid ads listed under Excluded?

## Traps

- **Scope by enthusiasm.** Adding channels the client didn't ask for as base deliverables. They are add-ons.
- **Units that can't be counted.** "Social media management" is not a deliverable; "12 LinkedIn posts a month" is.
- **Forgetting the destination.** Every piece drives traffic somewhere; if that place doesn't exist, building it is a setup deliverable.
