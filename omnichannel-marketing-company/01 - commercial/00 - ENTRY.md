# 01 - commercial

**Purpose:** everything between a request and paid, agreed work: understand the request, fix the scope, price it from the work itself, win the client's acceptance, invoice, and each month review results and offer more. The content department starts only after this department has agreed the scope and been paid its advance.

## How money works here

- **No price book.** Every price is derived from the job's own sub-tasks: effort (hours) × the client's rate for that kind of work, plus pass-through costs. So every invoice can be itemised down to the work.
- **What the client buys:** a proposal of a packaged retainer (or a one-off job) with add-ons. Scope and deliverables are fixed in it. Per-deliverable pricing keeps add-ons flexible; the retainer secures volume.
- **Rates:** set per client by the operator, one rate per kind of work (research, strategy, writing, visual prompting, video, publishing, account management), with the client's currency and tax treatment.
- **Sign-off:** the operator signs off every proposal before it reaches the client.
- **Payment:** setup upfront; each month 50% in advance, 50% on delivery.
- **Upsell:** proposed at the monthly review when results show room for more, or whenever the client asks.
