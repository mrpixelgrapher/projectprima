# 01 · Seeds

**Purpose:** extract six self-contained seeds from the passed article, and assign each to the channels that will develop it. A channel writer should be able to work from a seed without re-reading the article.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `06-drafting/02-article.md`, `05-research/04-spine.md`; `00-brief.md` (Channels and image slots); `03 - strategy/03 - channels/channel-roles.md` (the seed map).
- **Writes:** `08-shaping/01-seeds.md`.
- **Done when:** there are six seeds, each with a core and a development, and every channel in the brief has its seeds and angle assigned.

## Procedure

1. **Extract the six seeds.** Each gets a **core** (one sentence) and a **development** (2–3 sentences, with the particular that makes it concrete):

   | Seed | What it is | Where to find it |
   |---|---|---|
   | Core insight | The single most important claim | Spine part 1 (the one thing) |
   | Process breakdown | How the result is achieved, step by step | The movements that explain the method |
   | Contrarian angle | What this challenges, plus the evidence | Spine part 6 (the reader move: the wrong model) |
   | Framework | A named, reusable structure | A list, sequence or model in the article. Name it if it has no name |
   | Implication | What changes for the buyer because of this | The close |
   | Visual concept | Something that can be drawn | Any `[GRAPHIC: …]` slot, or the framework |

2. **Test each seed:** could a channel writer produce a post from it alone? If they would need the article to understand it, rewrite it.
3. **Assign the seeds.** For each channel in the brief, take its seeds from the seed map in `channel-roles.md`. When two channels share a seed, write each one's angle in one line (e.g. LinkedIn: "what HR should ask for"; email: "what we include with every order").

## Output template

    # Seeds — <task id>
    | Seed | Core (1 sentence) | Development (2–3 sentences) |
    ## Assignment
    | Channel | Seeds | Angle (if shared) |

## Worked example

Gemstone piece `real-or-fake` *(illustrative)*. Framework: "The 60-second check": 1) find the lab name on the report, 2) match the report number on the lab's site, 3) compare the carat weight. Assigned to LinkedIn (angle: what HR should ask any supplier) and to sales collateral (angle: how we make the check easy).

## Self-check

- [ ] Are all six seeds present, each with a core and a development?
- [ ] Is each seed usable without the article?
- [ ] Does every channel in the brief have seeds, with an angle wherever a seed is shared?

## Traps

- **Seeds that are headings.** "Authenticity" is a topic, not a seed.
- **Shared seeds without distinct angles.** That produces cross-posts.
