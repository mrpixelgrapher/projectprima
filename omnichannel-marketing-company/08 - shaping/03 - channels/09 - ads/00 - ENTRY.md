# Ads

**Purpose:** ad creative and copy variants for each ad platform in the plan, built to test angles against each other and to land on one page. Written only when the output family "ads" is IN at the frame (F3).

## Contract

- **Reads:** `08-shaping/01-seeds.md` (core insight, contrarian, proof points); `00-brief.md` (buyer, limits, CTA); the platforms listed in the channel plan (via CONTEXT).
- **Writes:** `08-shaping/03-channels/ads.md`.
- **Done when:** each platform has 3 primary texts, 3 headlines and 2 descriptions, a landing page, and its image slots.

## Procedure

1. **Per platform, three angles, one per primary text:**
   - **A:** the core insight;
   - **B:** the contrarian angle;
   - **C:** proof (a document in hand, or a result).
2. **Headlines (3) and descriptions (2)** that fit each angle.
3. **Character limits:** use the platform's current ad specs [VERIFY: query-bank Q12, checked this month]. Record the limit you used next to each field.
4. **Claims.** Ads carry only claims with proof in hand, and must respect the brief's Limits and the platform's ad policy for the category [VERIFY: the platform's policy]. Asserted-only claims are cut.
5. **Landing:** the website variant or the hub piece, whichever the channel plan names.
6. **Image slots:** `ad-<platform>-A/B/C`, one per angle, described for `04 - visuals/`.
7. **Running the ads** (budget, targeting, bids) is outside this pipeline, unless F4 = CONTENT+RUN was agreed. Note who runs them.

## Traps

- **Three rewordings of one angle.** Tests need different ideas.
- **Unprovable superlatives.** Policy rejections, and broken trust.
