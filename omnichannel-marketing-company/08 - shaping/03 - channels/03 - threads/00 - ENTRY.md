# Threads post

**Purpose:** a single native post that opens a discussion about what the piece's idea *changes* for the reader, and links to the hub.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `08-shaping/01-seeds.md` (the implication seed); `00-brief.md` (voice).
- **Writes:** `08-shaping/03-channels/threads.md`.
- **Done when:** the post meets every rule below.

## Procedure

1. **Open with the implication:** what changes for the reader. Never open with the process; that is the Twitter/X angle.
2. **Body.** Give 1–2 concrete examples of the implication in practice.
3. **Close with a question** that invites replies.
4. **Length:** at most 500 characters [VERIFY: the current limit, with query-bank Q12]. Count the characters and record the count in the metadata.
5. **Put the hub link directly in the post.**
6. **Image slot `threads-implication` is mandatory:** a graphic that shows the implication.
7. **Never the same angle as the Twitter/X thread** for the same piece.

## Output template

The metadata block (add `Characters: N`), then the post text.

## Worked example

*(illustrative)* "If your team can't verify a gift in a minute, you're gifting a claim, not a gemstone. What would change if every gift came with a check the recipient could do themselves? [hub link]"

## Self-check

- [ ] Is it ≤ 500 characters, with the count recorded?
- [ ] Does it open on the implication and close on a question?
- [ ] Does it have a `threads-implication` slot described?
- [ ] Is its angle different from the Twitter/X thread?

## Traps

- **A shortened thread.** Threads carries the *so what*, not the *how*.
