# Sales collateral

**Purpose:** copy for the one-pager (or catalogue entries) a buyer receives during a sale, turning the piece's framework and proof into reasons to order. Written only when "sales collateral" is IN at the frame, or approved at strategy.

## Contract

- **Reads:** `08-shaping/01-seeds.md` (framework, proof); `00-brief.md` (pillar, buyer, CTA, limits); the OFFER slot (via CONTEXT).
- **Writes:** `08-shaping/03-channels/sales-collateral.md`.
- **Done when:** the one-pager copy is complete, with every product fact sourced or marked CLIENT TO SUPPLY.

## Procedure

1. **Headline:** the promise from strategy. **Sub-headline:** who it's for.
2. **Three benefit blocks,** one per pillar. Each is a claim in the buyer's words, plus its proof (in hand only).
3. **The offer:** the range, the price band and the order process, using facts from the OFFER slot only. Anything missing becomes `[CLIENT TO SUPPLY: …]`.
4. **Proof block:** documents in hand (e.g. a lab report with every piece). Asserted-only claims are left out.
5. **CTA:** the brief's, with contact details as `[CLIENT TO SUPPLY]` unless they are known.
6. **Catalogue entries** (if the plan asks for them): one entry per product the client listed: name, material, size and price band, from client facts only.
7. **Image slots:** `collateral-cover`, plus one per product.

## Output template

    <metadata block>
    Headline / Sub-headline
    ## Benefits (3)
    ## The offer
    ## Proof
    ## CTA
    ## Catalogue (optional)

## Self-check

- [ ] Is every product fact from the OFFER slot, or marked CLIENT TO SUPPLY?
- [ ] Do the benefits carry only proof in hand?

## Traps

- **Inventing a range or a price.** It becomes a promise the client can't keep.
