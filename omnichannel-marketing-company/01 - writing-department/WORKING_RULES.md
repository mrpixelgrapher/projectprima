# Working Rules — Writing Department

These rules govern every execution of the Writing Department. A cold CE session reads these first and follows them deterministically.

## Rule 1: Research Before Drafting (mandatory)

No content drafting begins until the pre-drafting gate passes. See `.\..\..\..\..\02 - WORKSPACE_META\03 - folder-governance\RESEARCH_FIRST_CONTENT_PRODUCTION.md` for the full governance rule with enforcement checks and anti-pattern detection.

**Short form:**
1. Topic boundary must be mapped (3+ dimensions named)
2. Source depth must reach 3+ independent categories
3. Original synthesis must be possible from accumulated material
4. Research artifacts must exist on disk (not just in memory)

If any check fails, return to research. Do not draft.

## Rule 2: Research Artifacts Are Permanent (mandatory)

Every content-production task creates a research folder that survives beyond the article:

```
research-[topic-slug]/
├── sources/           ← Raw collected material
├── observations/      ← Extracted patterns, themes, contradictions
├── synthesis/         ← Original insights, frameworks, relationships
└── outputs/           ← Published content (apex layer)
```

Research artifacts are reusable intellectual capital. A future article on the same topic draws from the same research without re-acquisition.

## Rule 3: Drafting Is Synthesis, Not Discovery (mandatory)

The draft must be traceable to accumulated research. If a claim in the draft cannot be traced to a source, observation, or synthesis note, it was invented — reject it or add sourcing.

Drafting should be the SHORTEST stage. If drafting feels hard, research was insufficient.

## Rule 4: Content Quality Over Velocity (mandatory)

Do not accept content-production tasks that require publishing on a schedule without research depth. A content calendar does not create meaningful articles. Each article must be earned through accumulated research.

**Reject these requests:**
- "Write 5 articles this week" without 5 research domains ready
- "Publish daily for 30 days" without 30 days of accumulated research
- "Write about X by tomorrow" without research artifacts for X on disk

## Rule 5: AI Amplifies Research, Not Replaces It (mandatory)

AI is used for:
- Source discovery and pattern extraction (research phase)
- Structure, synthesis, and formatting (drafting phase)
- Traceability checking and terminology alignment (quality phase)

AI is NOT used for:
- Generating content without accumulated research
- Replacing the research phase with web search + summary
- Producing text that creates the illusion of understanding

## Rule 6: Platform Register Before Distribution (mandatory)

Before handing output to Content Distribution, verify:
- Article is published on Substack (the owned anchor)
- Decomposition seeds are extracted (core insight, process breakdown, contrarian angle, framework, implication, visual concept)
- Each seed is mapped to its target platform

## Rule 7: Collapse Rules Apply (mandatory)

All writing department output follows CE collapse rules:
- KEEP: directly responsive, grounded in files read, mechanically true, actionable
- REMOVE: speculative, aspirational, jargon-heavy, redundant, unsupported by local files
- OUTPUT STANDARD: exact paths, exact actions, short proofable statements
